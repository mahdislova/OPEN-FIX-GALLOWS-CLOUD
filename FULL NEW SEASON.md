# The Green Gallows — The Showrunner's Season

Oct 3, 2026 · @patrick

A new ten-episode season, built from scratch on the strongest bones of the original: the Harding legacy, Miriam and the Lost One, and the sister reveal.

## The pitch

London, November 1889, and the whole city glows green. Every lamp, tram and furnace burns gallowglass, a crystal that only House Harding knows how to grow, at the Harding Works on the old gallows field at Tyburn. The Hardings say it grows from iron in the ground. In fact it grows from a woman. For four hundred years they have kept a creature from the frozen world beyond, the White, chained under the Works, and they bleed her for light. The oath that holds her was sworn for four hundred winters, and it is fed with a life at the first snow every fifty years. The last life was Eldgard's own older sister. The next is due this winter, the four-hundredth, and it is the last the oath can hold.

**Series engine.** London runs on a secret. Every faction wants to own the secret, the girl at its centre or the boy who will inherit it. Every week, one plan for the light runs into another.

**Season question.** Can Nathaniel Harding end the bargain that lights London without becoming the father who kept it? Under it runs the sisters' question: will Alice Bounds let the thing wearing her dead sister's face choose its own life?

**Theme.** Protection as control. Every adult in this show hurts someone to keep them safe. The young ones have to learn whether love can let go.

**Tone.** The dread of *Chernobyl*, the sisters of *Arcane*, *House of Cards* in the drawing rooms. Whimsy breathes between the violence: a creature learning what an orange is, a recruit tangled in laundry lines, two engaged friends who plan to be very polite to each other for forty years.

**The clock.** Every episode opens on a card: 49 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW. Everyone assumes the first snow means the solstice, 21 December. At the end of Episode 8 the snow comes two weeks early, because the prisoner makes it fall, and the last two episodes play out over one night.

**Kept from your outline:** the Harding legacy and the 1489 massacre at the Gallows; Miriam's death and the Lost One; the sister reveal ("You're not her"); Josephine's protection turning into control; Eldgard's Snow Confession; Nixon's Hargrave blood; Tamara the operative; the Vykra; Ajax; Miriam's red mittens.

**Cut:** Edmund, Virgil, the Chapel, the Duke, the Prime Minister, Trevane. Each one did a job someone else now does, or existed only to deliver information. Morgana moves from a nun's body in the Chapel to the chain under the Works, so the villain's secret and the light's secret become the same secret.

**New:** Mother Grise, who runs the Vykra; Lord Chancellor Vane, for the Crown; and the Royal Lantern Guard as a full corps with its own gear, ranks and oath.

## The craft rules this season obeys

Eight rules, each taken from a writer you named, and each one checked against every episode below.

1. **Therefore and but, never "and then."** Each scene is caused by the last one or cuts across it. Parker and Stone's test: if the honest word between two beats is "and then," one of them goes. ([Perell](https://perell.com/note/but-therefore-rule/))
2. **Secrets with long fuses (Brent Weeks).** Weeks plots the character arcs and the big external climaxes, then layers in secrets he pays off books later. Here each major secret has three layers: what the public believes, what the audience learns, and what the character learns last, when it stings most. Everything serves his one rule: keep people turning pages. ([Reactor, Weeks AMA](https://reactormag.com/highlights-from-brent-weeks-r-fantasy-ama/))
3. **Plans collide.** Most set pieces happen because three or four characters have separate plans for the same night. Nobody's plan works as drawn. The explosion, the heist and the wolves in the streets all come out of choices, never coincidence.
4. **Sisters only trauma can split (Arcane).** Alex Yee: the sisters had to be "so emotionally entwined that only an event that traumatic" could drive a wedge between them. Alice and the Lost One get three episodes of closeness before the first wound and four before the break. ([SYFY, Alex Yee](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/arcane-co-creator-alex-yee-unpacks-the-act-i-twists?amp))
5. **Power lives inside intimate relationships (Willimon).** "Politics itself doesn't interest me. It's power that interests me." Every marriage, friendship and parent-child pair here is also a power arrangement, shown through behaviour and hands more than speeches. ([BFI notes](https://script-consultant.co.uk/bfi-beau-willimon-interview-notes-pt1/))
6. **One vision per episode.** Each episode has one focus you could put on its poster (the ball, the morgue, the hunt, the hearing, the heist), 20 to 22 scenes for about 44 minutes, and every scene turns something.
7. **The grid.** Your colleague's Want / Obstacle / Solution / Twist table runs for the four or five characters who carry each episode. The others rest and come back with their own stories later.
8. **Whimsy earns the grim.** Each episode alternates a light beat with a dark one, gives at least one feeling that goes unsaid, and gives one young character a chance to prove they belong. Every episode ends on a tag that changes the board: a reveal, an escalation, or a choice that can't be taken back.

## The world and its one rule

**The rule:** gallowglass is Morgana's frozen blood. Iron and Harding blood hold her chain. The oath runs four hundred winters, and every fiftieth first snow the chain must be fed with a life, or it breaks. For three centuries a hanged Hargrave sufficed. In 1839 the chain refused substitutes and demanded Harding blood. Alistair's son paid the founding, Eldgard's sister was the first Harding life the chain demanded since, and tonight's Tithe is the second, and the four-hundredth winter besides. If it breaks, she goes free, the light goes out for good, and the White comes through for one night.

Everything else follows from that sentence.

**The three layers of the secret**

| Layer | Who believes it | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | London | Gallowglass grows from iron under Tyburn. In 1489 the Hargrave rebels were hanged there for treason. |
| Second | Glim addicts, old Guardsmen, the Vykra | The light hums with a woman crying. People call it the Weeping and say it's the hanged. |
| Truth | Eldgard, later Nathaniel | Morgana, a mother from the White, has been chained under the Works since 1489. The Hargraves were a Harding cadet branch, hanged by their own cousins for trying to free her. Her daughter ran into the snow. |

**What gallowglass does**

- Burned in lamps and furnaces, it gives a green-white light and steady heat. The Ring, London's lamp grid, carries it under every street.
- Ground to dust it becomes **glim**, the undercity's drug. Users hear the Weeping, which is the first clue, given in Episode 1.
- It refuses anyone of the White unless they carry Harding blood, which is why the Lost One can touch Nathaniel without freezing him.

**The Royal Lantern Guard.** Chartered in 1689 to protect the Ring, they are London's elite, armed by the Harding Works. Oath: *Light the dark. Keep the flame.* Ranks run Wick (recruit), Flame (trooper), Lantern-Captain.

- **Lantern gauntlet:** a forearm rig with a gallowglass core. It fires a **flash-burst**, a blinding strobe that drops a man and sends wolves of the White running.
- **Leap-boosters:** steam and crystal thrust packs for rooftop flight, thirty seconds per charge. When the charge runs out, you fall.
- **Tether-hook:** a grapple line for swings and quick climbs.
- **Quench-blade:** a glass sabre that drinks heat, built for fast takedowns. One touch numbs a limb; a long cut freezes the blood.
- **Lantern rifle:** fires crystal rounds that burst into light. Every discharge goes into a log. That log matters in Episodes 4 and 5.

**Undercity tech.** **Ghostlights** are bootleg gauntlets, unstable and loud. Alice builds hers from parts stolen out of Nathaniel's shop. **Ajax's Black Gauntlet** is Nathaniel's own abandoned prototype, stolen two years ago, which hits like a piston.

**The Vykra.** A guild of undercity women, spies and knives for hire, run by Mother Grise from a Shadwell laundry. Their mark is a knot stitched inside the glove. Their poison, **winter's kiss**, is distilled from gallowglass and kills with frost on the lips, so it looks like the Frost Killer's work.

**The White.** A world of snow, wind and wolves on the far side of the chain. Time stops there. Whatever crosses over carries the cold with it. The Lost One kills by freezing when she's frightened, and each time she uses the cold she loses one of Miriam's memories.

**Places.** Harding House (Mayfair) and its glasshouse. The Harding Works over Tyburn, with the core deep below. Gin Lane tenements. Blackwood Bridge. The Shadwell laundry. Lambrick House and the Pimlico gasworks, where the Tyburn conduit (the old Works drain) runs under the city into the Thames. The Lantern Guard tower at Charing Cross. Parliament. Newgate.

## Lore: the past

The Hardings did not find a monster. They took a mother, and four centuries of light run on that. Alistair's son paid the founding in 1489, a Harding sister paid in 1839, and 1889 is the second cycle.

**Why fifty, and why it lands now.** 1489 plus four hundred is 1889, and 1839 plus fifty is 1889. The two clocks meet this winter. Morgana designed it that way: the oath ends here, so this is the one feeding she can break by being fed.

**Otty, in the season.** She is never seen. She appears three times: the brass on the chancel wall in Ep 2, the blue ribbon in Byron's pocket in Ep 6, and Eldgard's confession in Ep 9. By then the audience knows what Eldgard's "I raised Jamie for it" is a copy of.

**The Orchard.** Under the Works, thirteen steps below the coal-hatch stair, is a gallery of bunks and iron rings where Hargraves were kept alive for four hundred years: fed well, kept healthy, allowed up only to die. They were the house's spare blood, and the cattle in the contract. Hardings are taught never to say the word upstairs. By 1889 the herd is gone. Marsh's rhyme counts the seven who were left when he ran, and Nixon is the only Hargrave anyone ever brought out into the light.

**Tobias Marsh.** An Orchard man who ran in 1861 and was caught. The Crown holds him in Newgate as a madman, cell 1417, and nobody listens to what he sings. Jamie Harding visited him every Sunday for two years and learned the way down. The diagram in Jamie's boot-tree in Ep 2 has a stair of thirteen steps drawn beside the chain. That is why Jamie went to the Works with a plan in Ep 1, and why Nixon, tracing a dead boy's footsteps without knowing it, ends up in the Orchard in Ep 8.

**How Nixon learns it.**

1. Ep 3: his blood test glows green, and Eldgard pats his head: "Still good." Felix gives him the Newgate slip.
2. Ep 4: Marsh pinches his cheek, "Marian's chin!", and sings the rhyme. Nixon walks away not understanding it.
3. Ep 7: the file, "Reserve. Blood suitable." He learns he is the spare, not what the spare is for.
4. Ep 8: he follows the rhyme down thirteen steps and finds the Orchard and his mother's name. This is the scene that turns him.
5. Ep 9: Eldgard confirms it in the Snow Confession. Nixon frees Marsh from Newgate in the break-out.
6. Ep 10: he offers himself with his eyes open. "They grew me for this the way they grew my mother."

## Episode 1 — "The Engagement"

**49 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** 2 November 1889. **Focus:** one night, two parties: the Harding ball above, the glim run below, until the light flickers. **Set piece:** the Works blackout. **Sting:** the man who shoots the saboteur is the hero's best friend, and the saboteur is the hero's brother.

*Logline:* On the night his engagement is announced, Nathaniel Harding's brother dies at the family Works, his best friend fires the shot nobody can ever know about, and across the river a girl freezes solid on a bridge, then opens her eyes in the morgue.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nathaniel | To show his father the leap-gauntlet and earn a place in the work | Eldgard sees only Jamie. Inside, Nathaniel believes he's the spare | Demonstrates it at the ball anyway | Humiliated in public, then his brother is dead by morning |
| Alice | To pass her first Vykra test and to find out who Miriam keeps sneaking off to | The contract is a Harding. Her sister is lying to her | Gets into the ball as a lamp-girl | Finds Miriam frozen on the bridge, then sees her wake up |
| Nixon | To prove the charity-school boy deserves the captaincy | First command, a dark Works, a masked saboteur | Takes the shot | It's Jamie, and Eldgard makes him the keeper of the lie |
| Jamie | To break the chain and run with Miriam | His father, the Guard, and his own blood | Cuts the lock with a glass blade and his hand | Shot by his brother's best friend, left alone with his father |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **Tyburn, 1489. Night, no snow, but frost.** Three thousand Hargraves hang from a field of gallows. Below the ground, in a pit lined with iron, a woman of ice is chained by hooks. ALISTAIR HARDING, wolf-crest ring on his hand, opens her wrist, and her blood runs green and lights a lantern. *Fwhumph.* The first gallowglass light. Behind him a tiny girl made of frost slips out of the pit and runs into the white dark. The wolves follow her. Alistair doesn't look up.

**Act One**

2. **Under Gin Lane, the Ring tunnels.** 1889. ALICE and MIRIAM BOUNDS run glim through the lamp-mains. Alice splices a live Verdance line to black out a rival dealer's den, and shows off doing it. The buyer, mid-deal, tilts his head: "Hear that? Crying. Always crying with this batch." Miriam steals an orange from him, tosses it to Alice, laughs. Miriam wears a ring on a cord under her collar. *Therefore* Alice clocks it and says nothing.
3. **Harding Works, the gauntlet shop.** NATHANIEL straps on his leap-gauntlet prototype and fires the booster. *Kshhhh-THOOM.* He crosses the foundry in one jump and lands in a coal bin. BYRON, the butler, picks soot from his hair: "Very graceful, sir. The coal agrees." Nathaniel plans to show his father tonight.
4. **Harding House, the study.** ELDGARD and JAMIE. Eldgard: after tonight Jamie begins "his preparation." Jamie: "I know what the Tithe is." Eldgard pours two drinks. Jamie leaves his untouched. *But* neither man says the word that matters.
5. **Lantern Guard tower, Charing Cross.** NIXON HARGRAVE, 23, is made Lantern-Captain, the youngest in the corps. Eldgard, his patron since the charity school, pins the green-glass lantern to his collar: "My family looks after its own." COLE PRUETT, 17, a Wick, tries to salute and smacks himself in the eye.
6. **Vykra laundry, Shadwell.** Steam, lye, women folding knives into sheets. MOTHER GRISE gives Alice her first contract in a sealed envelope, from a client Grise won't name: the second Harding son. "Get close. Report everything." Alice has wanted this for three years. *Therefore* she has to get into the ball tonight.

**Act Two**

7. **Blackwood Bridge stair, dusk.** Alice tails Miriam and watches her climb into a coach and kiss JAMIE HARDING. A Harding! *But* the ball starts in an hour, and the Vykra don't give second tests.
8. **Harding House, the ballroom.** Arrivals under a thousand green chandeliers. LORD LAMBRICK, the coal baron, with his daughter TAMARA, who carries a cipher wheel inside her fan. Tamara and Nathaniel, engaged by their fathers, agree to be very polite to each other for forty years. Nixon, on duty at the door, watches Tamara and looks away when she looks back.
9. **The ballroom, service corridor.** Alice, in a lamp-girl's apron, lights wicks. Nathaniel's gauntlet shorts out and sprays sparks into a chandelier. Alice fixes it with a hairpin in four seconds. He stares: "Who taught you that?" "Nobody teaches lamp-girls anything, sir." A spark between them, and a lie under it.
10. **The ballroom.** The engagement is announced. Nathaniel steps forward to show his father the gauntlet. Eldgard, smiling for the room: "Not tonight. Toys after supper." Laughter. Nathaniel's face. Across the room, Jamie slips out early, and only Nathaniel sees him go.
11. **Gallery above the ballroom.** JOSEPHINE HARDING watches the lamp-girl. To Byron: "That girl holds her wrist like a knife-girl." Byron: "You'd know, madam." One look between them says they share thirty years of history and agreed never to mention it.
12. **The Works core, deep below.** Jamie descends with a stolen key. We glimpse the chained thing only as moving green light, and hear a woman weeping. Jamie cuts at the iron lock with a crystal blade until it sticks fast in the iron, then presses his bleeding palm to it. The chain shudders.

**Act Three**

13. **The ballroom.** Every chandelier gutters. Then London goes dark: street by street, the green dies across the city in a wave. In the black ballroom Nathaniel hears a woman crying inside the walls. Nobody else does. Eldgard is already gone.
14. **The Works, set piece.** Alarm: saboteur in the core. Nixon leads his squad down the gantries; Cole is with them. Flash-bursts strobe, *fzzt, fzzt*, freezing the dark into snapshots: pipes, steam, a masked figure at the lock. "Step away!" The figure turns, something glinting in his hand. Nixon fires his lantern rifle. *KRAK.* The figure drops.
15. **Blackwood Bridge, midnight.** Miriam waits in her red wool mittens. Jamie doesn't come. Frost creeps up the iron rail, then up her coat. She hears the Weeping. A small girl made of frost is standing beside her. Miriam, teeth chattering, asks her something we can't hear. The girl nods.
16. **The core.** Nixon pulls off the mask. It's Jamie, alive and bleeding. ELDGARD steps out of the dark: "Go, Captain. Wait at the gate. Speak to no one." Nixon goes. Eldgard kneels beside his son. *Cut away.* We don't see what he does.
17. **Blackwood Bridge.** Alice, still in her apron, finds Miriam frozen solid on the bridge, eyes open, inside a perfect ring of frost on a night with no frost anywhere else. Alice's scream. Grim and long.
18. **The Works gate.** Eldgard to Nixon: "Jamie fell. The saboteur escaped. You never saw his face. You'll be the man who kept this family's secret, and this family never forgets." Nixon nods. The leash goes on.
19. **Harding House, grey dawn.** Eldgard tells Josephine and Nathaniel: an accident in the core. Josephine looks at her husband a beat too long. Nathaniel hears the weeping again, and this time it's in the floor.
20. **The stables.** Nathaniel breaks down. Nixon holds him, his best friend's brother's blood still under his fingernails. *Therefore* every scene these two share from now on has a bomb under the table.

**Tag**

21. **City morgue.** INSPECTOR FELIX HAWKE pulls back the sheet. Alice identifies her sister. She touches Miriam's hand, and the frost melts under her fingers. *Drip. Drip.* The corpse's eyes move to Alice. "Ali." Cut to black.

## Episode 2 — "The Wake"

**47 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** what came back? **Set piece:** the morgue escape, which births the Frost Killer. **Sting:** Josephine hired the girl who is falling for her son.

*Logline:* Alice hides the thing that woke in her sister's body while London buries Jamie Harding. Nathaniel finds a Lantern Guard cartridge at the scene of his brother's "accident," and he asks the one man he trusts to find out who fired it, the man who fired it.

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **City morgue, minutes after Episode 1.** The thing in Miriam's body sits up and screams, a sound like glass splitting, *eeeeiiiKKK*. Two attendants freeze where they stand, mid-step. Felix's hand goes numb on his revolver. She runs barefoot into the night, and Alice runs after her.

**Act One**

2. **Gin Lane rooftops.** Alice follows a trail of frost from roof to roof. She finds her "sister" curled inside a lamp tower, hands cupped round the crystal, draining its light to get warm. Alice talks her down with their childhood counting game. The Lost One answers in Miriam's words, slightly wrong. Alice takes her home.

"Sons." In the doorway behind him, his firstborn, Edric, nine years old, holds the lantern for his father, then carries it down the stair ahead of him, alone. The same choice Nathaniel faces tonight.

**Act One**

2. **The core, midnight.** The lever drops. The Heartglass floods the Lost One with green fire, and she screams. Then it cracks, as Eldgard said it would. The chain shudders and doesn't take. Lambrick, from the gallery: "Then the boy! Take the Harding!" Vane's marshals start down the stairs.
3. **The core floor.** Alice smashes the Heartglass with her gauntlet, *KSSHHH*, and drags the Lost One out, half turned to crystal. Josephine draws her knives and they fight, Vykra against Vykra, the mother against the girl she paid to love her son. Josephine is better. Nathaniel steps between them: "Mother."
4. **The core floor.** Nixon comes down the stair bleeding, with Tamara behind him. "I'm Harding blood. Take me. They grew me for this the way they grew my mother. Let me be something on purpose." Cole: "Sir, no."
5. **The lock.** Nathaniel: "No one goes in the chain again." Jamie's glass blade is still frozen into the lock where he left it on the first night. Nathaniel pulls it free, cuts his palm, and presses his blood and his father's wolf ring into the iron. Harding blood closed it, and Harding blood opens it. Somewhere above, a church bell strikes twelve.
6. **London.** The chain falls. Every light in London goes out, and this time for good. One wordless shot after another: a tram dying in the snow, a hospital ward going black, a mother on Gin Lane lighting a tallow candle with shaking hands.

**Act Two**

7. **The core.** Morgana rises out of the pit as her wounds close, nine feet tall. She looks down at Nathaniel: "A Harding opened it." She doesn't thank him. The White pours through behind her, with wind, snow and wolves, and she goes up through the Works roof toward Tyburn hill.
8. **The Works vault.** Lambrick is stuffing the last gallowglass into satchels; it's the most valuable thing left on earth. Tamara finds him and offers her hand: "Come out, Father." He looks at the crystals. He looks at her. He chooses the crystals. She walks out. Behind her the wolves come into the vault.
9. **The core stair.** Nixon against Ajax: quench-blade against Black Gauntlet. Nixon takes the quick-takedown: blade into the gauntlet's crystal, which freezes the piston, then into Ajax. He dies frozen mid-laugh, his apple rolling down the stairs.
10. **London, the longest night.** Nixon's forty and every loyal Lantern form flash-lines across the bridges to keep the wolves off the tenements. The Vykra hold Gin Lane with knives and candles. Tamara throws open every Lambrick coal depot free to the city, and there are fires in every street. Whimsy in the dark: Cole teaches a crowd of children to make flash-shadow rabbits on the walls.
11. **Blackfriars Bridge.** The charges run out along Cole's line. He holds it with his last cartridge, two fingers short and seventeen years old, until it's empty. Nixon finds him at dawn's edge, sitting up against the parapet as if he were resting, his diary open in his lap to the burned log page.
12. **The core, empty.** Nathaniel and Josephine alone. "I'd do it all again." "I know. That's why I can't be you."

**Act Three**

13. **Tyburn hill, set piece.** Morgana stands over London in the storm, with wolves in a ring around her. Four hundred years of grievance. The young alliance climbs to her: Nathaniel, and Alice carrying the Lost One. Josephine follows.
14. **Tyburn hill.** The Lost One stands between her mother and the city. "They let you go. Let them go." Morgana: "They bled me for four hundred years." "One of them let you go."
15. **Tyburn hill.** Morgana: "Then come home, little frost." The Lost One looks at Alice. Alice, who has spent the season holding on, lets go: "Go, if you want to. I'll be all right." She means it.
16. **Tyburn hill.** The Lost One tells her the last thing: "On the bridge she was still alive. She asked me to go back to Ali. I said yes. Then I wanted to." Miriam's last thought was Alice, after all. The Lost One stays. Morgana accepts, and names her price: "A Harding chained me. A Harding comes with me." Nathaniel steps forward.
17. **Tyburn hill.** Josephine steps past him. "I'm a Harding. I chose the name." She kisses his forehead. Morgana studies her: "He would spare you least of all." "Yes." To Alice: "Keep him out of the Works." It's the contract she paid for in Episode 2, now given as a blessing. She takes Morgana's hand and they walk into the white. The wolves follow, and the storm goes with them.
18. **Dawn.** The snow stops. London lies dark and white, coal smoke rising from Tamara's fires. Silence, then birds.
19. **Westminster.** Vane names Nathaniel "the man who put out London" and signs a warrant. Tamara, Lady Harding and heir to Lambrick, puts the Heartglass folio on his desk, with the Crown's seal on the binding order. Nathaniel's pardon is signed within the hour. She now owns the coal, which means she owns the city's heat. Nixon refuses the lantern Vane offers him: "The Guard will keep its own flame."
20. **Guard tower roof.** Nixon and Tamara. "Married twelve hours." "Twenty-two, now." Her hand stays on his. Nothing is decided.
21. **Harding House, cold and dark.** Nathaniel, head of a house with no light, in a city that hates him. Alice: "Your mother hired me to keep you out of the Works." "Did it work?" "No." A small smile, and no kiss. He drops the wolf ring into the dead grate. Alice sets Jamie's ring, the one from Miriam's cord, on the mantel beside it. Neither of them says a word.

**Tag**

22. **Gin Lane, morning.** The Lost One is warm for the first time and sneezes, astonished. Then she's sick in a basin, exactly like Miriam in Episode 7's cold open. Alice laughs, then stops, and puts her hand on her sister's belly. A heartbeat. The White kept Miriam's body frozen in time, and now time runs again. Jamie's child. The Lost One: "Is that me?" Alice: "No. That's someone else."

**Post-credits**

23. **The White.** Endless snow. Josephine walks beside Morgana and stops at a figure standing in the drifts, where the chain's dead went: JAMIE HARDING, frost in his hair, breathing. "Mother?"

**What Season 2 inherits**

- **A London with no light.** Coal is power now, and Tamara owns the coal. The young woman who wanted to be her father's heir has become something more dangerous.
- **A Harding child carried by a daughter of the White.** Both worlds will want it.
- **Jamie, alive in the White** with his mother and Morgana. The father of that child is on the wrong side of the world.
- **Nixon and Tamara**: married to his cousin, holding his hand, nothing decided. Nathaniel and Alice: no kiss, no promise, a dead grate between them.
