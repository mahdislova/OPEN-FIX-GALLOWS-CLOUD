# The Green Gallows — The Showrunner's Season

Oct 3, 2026 · @patrick

A new ten-episode season, built from scratch on the strongest bones of the original: the Harding legacy, Miriam and the Lost One, and the sister reveal.

## The pitch

London, November 1889, and the whole city glows green. Every lamp, tram and furnace burns gallowglass, a crystal that only House Harding knows how to grow, at the Harding Works on the old gallows field at Tyburn. The Hardings say it grows from iron in the ground. In fact it grows from a woman. For four hundred years they have kept a creature from the frozen world beyond, the White, chained under the Works, and they bleed her for light. The oath that holds her was sworn for four hundred winters, and it is fed with a life at the first snow every fifty years. The last life was Eldgard's own older sister. The next is due this winter, the four-hundredth, and it is the last the oath can hold.

**Series engine.** London runs on a secret. Every faction wants to own the secret, the girl at its centre or the boy who will inherit it. Every week, one plan for the light runs into another.

**Season question.** Can Nathaniel Harding end the bargain that lights London without becoming the father who kept it? Under it runs the sisters' question: will Alice Bounds let the thing wearing her dead sister's face choose its own life?

**Theme.** Protection as control. Every adult in this show hurts someone to keep them safe. The young ones have to learn whether love can let go.

**Tone.** The dread of *Chernobyl*, the sisters of *Arcane*, *House of Cards* in the drawing rooms. Whimsy breathes between the violence: a creature learning what an orange is, a recruit tangled in laundry lines, two engaged friends who plan to be very polite to each other for forty years.

**The clock.** Every episode opens on a card: 49 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW. Everyone assumes the first snow means the solstice, 21 December. At the end of Episode 8 the snow comes two weeks early, because the prisoner makes it fall, and the last two episodes play out over one night.

**Kept from your outline:** the Harding legacy and the 1489 massacre at the Gallows; Miriam's death and the Lost One; the sister reveal ("You're not her"); Josephine's protection turning into control; Eldgard's Snow Confession; Nixon's Hargrave blood; Tamara the operative; the Vykra; Ajax; Miriam's red mittens.

**Cut:** Edmund, Virgil, the Chapel, the Duke, the Prime Minister, Trevane. Each one did a job someone else now does, or existed only to deliver information. Morgana moves from a nun's body in the Chapel to the chain under the Works, so the villain's secret and the light's secret become the same secret.

**New:** Mother Grise, who runs the Vykra; Lord Chancellor Vane, for the Crown; and the Royal Lantern Guard as a full corps with its own gear, ranks and oath.

## The craft rules this season obeys

Eight rules, each taken from a writer you named, and each one checked against every episode below.

1. **Therefore and but, never "and then."** Each scene is caused by the last one or cuts across it. Parker and Stone's test: if the honest word between two beats is "and then," one of them goes. ([Perell](https://perell.com/note/but-therefore-rule/))
2. **Secrets with long fuses (Brent Weeks).** Weeks plots the character arcs and the big external climaxes, then layers in secrets he pays off books later. Here each major secret has three layers: what the public believes, what the audience learns, and what the character learns last, when it stings most. Everything serves his one rule: keep people turning pages. ([Reactor, Weeks AMA](https://reactormag.com/highlights-from-brent-weeks-r-fantasy-ama/))
3. **Plans collide.** Most set pieces happen because three or four characters have separate plans for the same night. Nobody's plan works as drawn. The explosion, the heist and the wolves in the streets all come out of choices, never coincidence.
4. **Sisters only trauma can split (Arcane).** Alex Yee: the sisters had to be "so emotionally entwined that only an event that traumatic" could drive a wedge between them. Alice and the Lost One get three episodes of closeness before the first wound and four before the break. ([SYFY, Alex Yee](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/arcane-co-creator-alex-yee-unpacks-the-act-i-twists?amp))
5. **Power lives inside intimate relationships (Willimon).** "Politics itself doesn't interest me. It's power that interests me." Every marriage, friendship and parent-child pair here is also a power arrangement, shown through behaviour and hands more than speeches. ([BFI notes](https://script-consultant.co.uk/bfi-beau-willimon-interview-notes-pt1/))
6. **One vision per episode.** Each episode has one focus you could put on its poster (the ball, the morgue, the hunt, the hearing, the heist), 17 to 22 scenes for about 44 minutes, and every scene turns something. The three tent-poles (Ep 5, the midseason heist; Ep 8, the wedding; Ep 10, the finale) run 21 to 22 so their set pieces have room. The rest run 17 to 19, with scenes that only carry information folded into scenes that carry feeling.
7. **The grid.** Your colleague's Want / Obstacle / Solution / Twist table runs for the four or five characters who carry each episode. The others rest and come back with their own stories later.
8. **Whimsy earns the grim.** Each episode alternates a light beat with a dark one, gives at least one feeling that goes unsaid, and gives one young character a chance to prove they belong. Every episode ends on a tag that changes the board: a reveal, an escalation, or a choice that can't be taken back.

## The world and its one rule

**The rule:** gallowglass is Morgana's frozen blood. Iron and Harding blood hold her chain. The oath runs four hundred winters, and every fiftieth first snow the chain must be fed with a life, or it breaks. For three centuries a hanged Hargrave sufficed. In 1839 the chain refused the hanged and the herded, and demanded what Alistair first gave it: a Harding who walks down the stair of their own will. Blood taken by force only buys time. That is why Jamie, held to the lock against his will, bought forty-nine days and not fifty years. Alistair's son paid the founding, Eldgard's sister was the first Harding life the chain demanded since, and tonight's Tithe is the second, and the four-hundredth winter besides. If it breaks, she goes free, the light goes out for good, and the White comes through for one night.

Everything else follows from that sentence.

**The three layers of the secret**

| Layer | Who believes it | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | London | Gallowglass grows from iron under Tyburn. In 1489 the Hargrave rebels were hanged there for treason. |
| Second | Glim addicts, old Guardsmen, the Vykra | The light hums with a woman crying. People call it the Weeping and say it's the hanged. |
| Truth | Eldgard, later Nathaniel | Morgana, a mother from the White, has been chained under the Works since 1489. The Hargraves were a Harding cadet branch, hanged by their own cousins for trying to free her. Her daughter ran into the snow. |

**What gallowglass does**

- Burned in lamps and furnaces, it gives a green-white light and steady heat. The Ring, London's lamp grid, carries it under every street.
- Ground to dust it becomes **glim**, the undercity's drug. Users hear the Weeping, which is the first clue, given in Episode 1. It is Morgana's lullaby to her daughter, and London has sung it for four hundred years as the Lantern Hymn, with new words nailed over the old tune.
- It refuses anyone of the White unless they carry Harding blood, which is why the Lost One can touch Nathaniel without freezing him.

**The Royal Lantern Guard.** Chartered in 1689 to protect the Ring, they are London's elite, armed by the Harding Works. Oath: *Light the dark. Keep the flame.* Ranks run Wick (recruit), Flame (trooper), Lantern-Captain.

- **Lantern gauntlet:** a forearm rig with a gallowglass core. It fires a **flash-burst**, a blinding strobe that drops a man and sends wolves of the White running.
- **Leap-boosters:** steam and crystal thrust packs for rooftop flight, thirty seconds per charge. When the charge runs out, you fall.
- **Tether-hook:** a grapple line for swings and quick climbs.
- **Quench-blade:** a glass sabre that drinks heat, built for fast takedowns. One touch numbs a limb; a long cut freezes the blood.
- **Lantern rifle:** fires crystal rounds that burst into light. Every discharge goes into a log. That log matters in Episodes 4 and 5.

**Undercity tech.** **Ghostlights** are bootleg gauntlets, unstable and loud. Alice builds hers from parts stolen out of Nathaniel's shop. **Ajax's Black Gauntlet** is Nathaniel's own abandoned prototype, stolen two years ago, which hits like a piston.

**The Vykra.** A guild of undercity women, spies and knives for hire, run by Mother Grise from a Shadwell laundry. Their mark is a knot stitched inside the glove. Their poison, **winter's kiss**, is distilled from gallowglass and kills with frost on the lips, so it looks like the Frost Killer's work.

**The White.** A world of snow, wind and wolves on the far side of the chain. Time stops there. Whatever crosses over carries the cold with it. The Lost One kills by freezing when she's frightened, and each time she uses the cold she loses one of Miriam's memories.

**Places.** Harding House (Mayfair) and its glasshouse. The Harding Works over Tyburn, with the core deep below. Gin Lane tenements. Blackwood Bridge. The Shadwell laundry. Lambrick House and the Pimlico gasworks, where the Tyburn conduit (the old Works drain) runs under the city into the Thames. The Lantern Guard tower at Charing Cross. Parliament. Newgate.

**Why fifty, and why it lands now.** 1489 plus four hundred is 1889, and 1839 plus fifty is 1889. The two clocks meet this winter. Morgana designed it that way: the oath ends here, so this is the one feeding she can break by being fed.

**Otty, in the season.** She is never seen. She appears four times: the brass on the chancel wall in Ep 2, Eldgard's "She told me my sister was still singing" in Ep 5, the blue ribbon in Byron's pocket in Ep 6, and Eldgard's confession in Ep 9. By then the audience knows what Eldgard's "I raised Jamie for it" is a copy of.

**The Orchard.** Under the Works, thirteen steps below the coal-hatch stair, is a gallery of bunks and iron rings where Hargraves were kept alive for four hundred years: fed well, kept healthy, allowed up only to die. They were the house's spare blood, and the cattle in the contract. Hardings are taught never to say the word upstairs. By 1889 the herd is gone. Marsh's rhyme counts the seven who were left when he ran, and Nixon is the only Hargrave anyone ever brought out into the light.

**Tobias Marsh.** An Orchard man who ran in 1861 and was caught. The Crown holds him in Newgate as a madman, cell 1417, and nobody listens to what he sings. Jamie Harding visited him every Sunday for two years and learned the way down. The diagram in Jamie's boot-tree in Ep 2 has a stair of thirteen steps drawn beside the chain. That is why Jamie went to the Works with a plan in Ep 1, and why Nixon, tracing a dead boy's footsteps without knowing it, ends up in the Orchard in Ep 8.

**How Nixon learns it.**

1. Ep 3: his blood test glows green, and Eldgard pats his head: "Still good." Felix gives him the Newgate slip.
2. Ep 4: Marsh pinches his cheek, "Marian's chin!", and sings the rhyme. Nixon walks away not understanding it.
3. Ep 7: the file, "Reserve. Blood suitable." He learns he is the spare, not what the spare is for.
4. Ep 8: he follows the rhyme down thirteen steps and finds the Orchard and his mother's name. This is the scene that turns him.
5. Ep 9: Eldgard confirms it in the Snow Confession. Nixon frees Marsh from Newgate in the break-out.
6. Ep 10: he offers himself with his eyes open. "They grew me for this the way they grew my mother."

### The wound under the sisters

When Alice and Miriam were small, their stepfather drank and kept a belt. Miriam, four years older, made Alice a promise in the dark under their bed: *nothing will ever touch you while I'm here.* Then the belt came out, and Miriam didn't move. She never once moved. What she did was teach Alice to count: the posts of the fence outside the window, one to eight, over and over, until it was over. "Eight posts and he's done." That's the counting game. The sisters have played it as a joke for fifteen years, and neither of them has ever said where it came from. The stepfather went into the river one winter, drunk, and nobody looked for him.

Alice keeps all of it **behind the eightfold fence**, her own phrase, the one she uses when anyone gets close: *that's behind the fence.* It's the root of her lie. She learned that love is holding on, because nobody held the line for her.

Miriam carried it differently: guilt, all of it, never said. When the Lost One comes through on the bridge and puts Miriam on like a coat, it gets the guilt with the memories. A creature that has never felt anything human now has fifteen years of a sister's shame, and no idea what to do with it.

**How the show handles it.** The abuse is never shown. It lives in sound (a buckle, a floorboard), in Alice's flinch, and in the counting. It's about what it did to two girls, not about the man.

**The irony at the heart of the sisters' story.** Miriam knew she owed Alice an apology and never gave it. The Lost One isn't Miriam, but she gives it, and that's how Alice knows her sister is dead.

### How the season stays producible

Morgana is a practical performer in prosthetics from the waist up, with CG for the ice and fire and the full nine feet saved for Eps 5, 8 and 10. The pilot shows only her hand. The wolves are shown the way the flash-bursts show everything: one strobe frame at a time, so we render frames, not sequences, and the audience's imagination does the rest. Ep 4 (a hearing room and a dock) and Ep 7's Pavilion (one interior, a hundred extras and a song) are the season's contained episodes, which pays for the Newgate break-out and Tyburn hill.

## Episode 1 — "The Engagement"

**49 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** 2 November 1889. **Focus:** one night, two parties: the Harding ball above, the glim run below, until the light flickers. **Set piece:** the Works blackout. **Sting:** the man who shoots the saboteur is the hero's best friend, and the saboteur is the hero's brother.

*Logline:* On the night his engagement is announced, Nathaniel Harding's brother dies at the family Works, his best friend fires the shot nobody can ever know about, and across the river a girl freezes solid on a bridge, then opens her eyes in the morgue.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **Tyburn, 1489. Night, no snow, but frost.** The Crown's gallows field, a hundred empty frames and one old thief turning on his rope. *Creak. Creak.* Below the ground, in a pit lined with iron, something vast breathes behind the lantern glare. We never see her face. We see a hand of blue ice the size of a cartwheel with an iron hook through it, and we hear her singing: a wordless lullaby, low and patient, a tune the audience will hear again without knowing where. ALISTAIR HARDING, wolf-crest ring on his hand, opens her wrist, and her blood runs green into a lantern. *Fwhumph.* The first gallowglass light. A boy of nine holds the lantern up for his father, solemn and proud to be useful. In the dark behind them a tiny girl made of frost slips out of the pit. Alistair sees her, and steps aside to let her pass. He has what he wanted. She runs into the white dark, and the wolves follow her. At the treeline a young woman in Harding colours, nineteen, a cousin, has seen all of it. She backs into the trees before anyone sees her face. The singing doesn't stop.

**Act One**

2. **Under Gin Lane, the Ring tunnels.** 1889. ALICE and MIRIAM BOUNDS run glim through the lamp-mains. Alice splices a live Verdance line to black out a rival dealer's den, and shows off doing it. The buyer, mid-deal, tilts his head: "Hear that? Crying. Always crying with this batch." Then he pulls his belt loose to tie off a leaking pipe, and the buckle rings on the iron. Alice is across the tunnel before she knows she's moved. Miriam sees it, and looks away. Then she steals an orange from him, tosses it to Alice and laughs, and the moment is gone. It's Alice's twentieth birthday. She waits all scene for Miriam to say so. Miriam doesn't; she's somewhere else. Miriam wears a ring on a cord under her collar. *Therefore* Alice clocks it and says nothing.
3. **Harding Works, the gauntlet shop.** NATHANIEL straps on his leap-gauntlet prototype and fires the booster. *Kshhhh-THOOM.* He crosses the foundry in one jump and lands in a coal bin. BYRON, the butler, picks soot from his hair: "Very graceful, sir. The coal agrees." Nathaniel plans to show his father tonight.
4. **Harding House, the study.** ELDGARD and JAMIE. Eldgard: after tonight Jamie begins "his preparation." Jamie: "I know what the Tithe is." Eldgard pours two drinks. Jamie leaves his untouched. *But* neither man says the word that matters.
5. **Lantern Guard tower, Charing Cross.** NIXON HARGRAVE, 23, is made Lantern-Captain, the youngest in the corps. Eldgard, his patron since the charity school, pins the green-glass lantern to his collar: "My family looks after its own." COLE PRUETT, 17, a Wick, tries to salute and smacks himself in the eye.

"Get close. Report everything." Alice has wanted this for three years. *Therefore* she has to get into the ball tonight.

**Act Two**

7. **Blackwood Bridge stair, dusk.** Alice tails Miriam and watches her climb into a coach and kiss JAMIE HARDING. A Harding! *But* the ball starts in an hour, and the Vykra don't give second tests.
8. **Harding House, the ballroom.** Arrivals under a thousand green chandeliers. LORD LAMBRICK, the coal baron, with his daughter TAMARA, who carries a cipher wheel inside her fan. Tamara and Nathaniel, engaged by their fathers, agree to be very polite to each other for forty years. Nixon, on duty at the door, watches Tamara and looks away when she looks back.

Alice fixes it with a hairpin in four seconds, and under her breath, so low only he could hear it, she counts them: "One post, two post, three, four." He stares: "Who taught you that?" "Nobody teaches lamp-girls anything, sir." A spark between them, and a lie under it. He doesn't ask about the counting. He keeps it.

To Byron: "That girl holds her wrist like a knife-girl." Byron: "You'd know, madam." One look between them says they share thirty years of history and agreed never to mention it. Below them, Nathaniel steps forward to show his father the gauntlet. Eldgard, smiling for the room: "Not tonight. Toys after supper." Laughter. Nathaniel's face. Across the room, Jamie slips out early, and only Nathaniel sees him go.

**Act Three**

12. **The ballroom.** Every chandelier gutters. Then London goes dark: street by street, the green dies across the city in a wave. In the black ballroom Nathaniel hears a woman crying inside the walls. Nobody else does. Eldgard is already gone.
13. **The Works, set piece.** Alarm: saboteur in the core. Nixon leads his squad down the gantries; Cole is with them. Flash-bursts strobe, *fzzt, fzzt*, freezing the dark into snapshots: pipes, steam, a masked figure at the lock. "Step away!" The figure turns, something glinting in his hand. Nixon fires his lantern rifle. *KRAK.* The figure drops.
14. **Blackwood Bridge, midnight.** Miriam waits in her red wool mittens. Jamie doesn't come. Frost creeps up the iron rail, then up her coat. She hears the Weeping. A small girl made of frost is standing beside her. Miriam, teeth chattering, asks her something we can't hear. The girl nods.
15. **The core.** Nixon pulls off the mask. It's Jamie, alive and bleeding. ELDGARD steps out of the dark: "Go, Captain. Wait at the gate. Speak to no one." Nixon goes. On the gantry above, forgotten, COLE watches the man on the floor lift his head and say something to his father. Eldgard kneels beside his son. *Cut away.* We don't see what he does.
16. **Blackwood Bridge.** Alice, still in her apron, finds Miriam frozen solid on the bridge, eyes open, inside a perfect ring of frost on a night with no frost anywhere else. Alice's scream. Grim and long.
17. **The Works gate.** Eldgard to Nixon: "Jamie fell. The saboteur escaped. You never saw his face. You'll be the man who kept this family's secret, and this family never forgets." Nixon nods. The leash goes on.
18. **Harding House, grey dawn, then the stables.** Eldgard tells Josephine and Nathaniel: an accident in the core. Josephine looks at her husband a beat too long. Nathaniel hears the weeping again, and this time it's in the floor. He makes it as far as the stables before he breaks. Nixon finds him there and holds him, his best friend's brother's blood still under his fingernails. *Therefore* every scene these two share from now on has a bomb under the table.

**Tag**

19. **City morgue.** INSPECTOR FELIX HAWKE pulls back the sheet. Alice identifies her sister. She touches Miriam's hand, and the frost melts under her fingers. *Drip. Drip.* The corpse's eyes move to Alice. "Ali." Cut to black.

## Episode 2 — "The Wake"

**47 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** what came back? **Set piece:** the morgue escape, which births the Frost Killer. **Sting:** Josephine hired the girl who is falling for her son.

*Logline:* Alice hides her sister, who sat up on a morgue slab and isn't quite right, and decides it's a miracle, while London buries Jamie Harding. Nathaniel finds a Lantern Guard cartridge at the scene of his brother's "accident," and he asks the one man he trusts to find out who fired it, the man who fired it.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alice | To get Miriam well | She kills by touch, she doesn't remember things, and the police say she was dead | Takes her to Old Wren, who calls it shock and amnesia. Alice believes her and sets rules | She's been asked to get close to Nathaniel, and now she wants to |
| The Lost One | To get warm and find the crying | She doesn't know how to be a person | Copies Miriam's words and habits, and lets Alice call her Mim | Touches a cat, and it dies |
| Nathaniel | To see his brother's body | Closed coffin, his father's orders | Searches the core level himself | Finds a Guard cartridge and hands the case to Nixon |
| Nixon | To bury the night | Cole saw the shot, and now Nathaniel wants answers | Silences Cole kindly | Assigned to investigate himself |
| Josephine | To keep Nathaniel out of the Works | Jamie is dead, and Nathaniel is the last son | Pays Grise in full and doubles the contract | The girl she hired is the lamp-girl her son can't stop looking at |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **City morgue, minutes after Episode 1.** The thing in Miriam's body sits up and screams, a sound like glass splitting, *eeeeiiiKKK*. Two attendants freeze where they stand, mid-step. Felix's hand goes numb on his revolver. She runs barefoot into the night, and Alice runs after her.

**Act One**

2. **Gin Lane rooftops.** Alice follows a trail of frost from roof to roof. She finds her "sister" curled inside a lamp tower, hands cupped round the crystal, draining its light to get warm. Alice talks her down with their childhood counting game, "one post, two post," and the creature counts back in Miriam's voice, because the body knows it even if she doesn't. Before dawn Alice hammers on OLD WREN's door. Wren, who set Alice's arm when she was nine, listens to a heart that's barely there and holds a mirror under a breath that barely fogs it. "Ice does this. I've seen a drowned boy sit up after an hour by the fire. She's had a shock, love. The memories come back in their own time." She sees more than she says, and Alice's face begs her not to say it. Alice takes her sister home, and chooses to believe.
3. **Harding House, the parlour.** Jamie lies in a closed coffin. Nathaniel insists on seeing him; Eldgard refuses. Byron gently moves Nathaniel's hand off the lid, and Nathaniel notices that Byron's own hands are shaking.
4. **Lantern Guard tower.** Nixon files his first report as captain: a saboteur fled. Cole, cheerful: "I saw you hit him, sir. Flash went right through him." Nixon: "You saw a flash, Wick. Flashes lie." Cole decides to believe him. *But* that night he writes all of it in his diary anyway: the flash, the shoulder, and the man on the floor still talking when the Captain was sent away.
5. **Gin Lane tenement.** Whimsy. The Lost One meets bread, a kettle (terror), and a cat (delight). She calls Alice "Ali" exactly the way Miriam did. Then she picks up the cat and it freezes stiff in her arms. Her face: she doesn't understand death. Alice does, and decides it's the cold still in her from the bridge, the way Wren said. *Therefore* rules: gloves, no touching, until it passes. Miriam's red mittens go on her hands. Alice: "You'll be yourself again soon, Mim." The Lost One looks at her a long moment, a creature deciding whether to tell the truth to the only warm thing in the world. "Yes, Ali." The first lie, and the first kindness.

**Act Two**

6. **Lambrick coach.** Lambrick to Tamara: "The heir is dead. Your fiancé is the heir now. Marry him fast." Tamara: "Let me run the books instead." "You're a wedding, not a ledger." On the ride she reads his account book upside down and spots a line: *2 Nov, Works gate, 200 gns, A.F.*
7. **St Bride's, the funeral.** The Lantern Guard honour guard; Nixon carries the coffin, and it's wrong in his hands: heavier than a man, and dead-still on the steps, nothing settling. He tells himself that's what guilt weighs. Lord Chancellor VANE and the great houses attend. Nathaniel gives the eulogy and breaks halfway. When the church lamps flare he hears the Weeping under the hymn, the same tune a half-step down. Among the Harding brasses on the chancel wall is a girl's: OTTOLINE HARDING, 1820 TO 1839, DIED ABROAD. Nathaniel asks Byron who she was. "Your father's sister, sir." Byron says no more, and Eldgard never turns his head toward it. Tamara takes his hand: kindness, nothing more. Nixon watches.
8. **The graveside.** Alice is there on her contract, posing as a mourner from the Works, and for herself: Miriam loved this man's brother. Nathaniel recognises the lamp-girl. Two people grieving, one of them lying. He offers his handkerchief, the only thing he has done all day that nobody told him to do; she keeps it.
9. **Vykra laundry.** Josephine, veiled, meets Grise and pays in gold for the contract she placed weeks ago on her son, and doubles it now that Jamie is dead. "Keep him close. Keep him out of the Works. His father will use him." A reveal for the audience: Josephine is old Vykra, and Alice's contract came from Nathaniel's mother.
10. **Harding Works, core level.** Nathaniel forces the outer gate. The core door is sealed and Eldgard's men turn him back, but on the stairs he finds a spent Lantern Guard rifle cartridge. *Therefore* his brother's death wasn't an accident.
11. **Guard tower roof.** Nathaniel shows Nixon the cartridge: "This is one of yours." Nixon: "Half the corps was down there after." "Then find me the one who was down there before." Nixon takes the case. He's now investigating himself.

**Act Three**

12. **Tenement.** The Lost One keeps drawing a chain on the frosted window and whispering "Mother." Alice, gently, wiping it away: "Mum's gone, Mim. Ten years." The Lost One lets her think that's who she meant. Round her neck, under the mittens' cord, Alice finds Jamie's ring. The Lost One: "He was supposed to come." *Therefore* Alice needs to know why he didn't.
13. **Harding House, Jamie's bedroom, night.** Alice breaks in Vykra-quiet and finds Nathaniel already there, searching. A standoff in the dark ends with the truth, almost: "My sister loved your brother. She nearly died the same night he did." Nathaniel didn't know Jamie loved anyone. Then the door handle turns. There's one place to go: Jamie's wardrobe, among his coats, chest to chest in the dark, his heart going against her shoulder. Through the slats, ELDGARD comes in alone with a candle, sits on his dead son's bed, and winds Jamie's pocket watch. *Tk-tk-tk-tk.* He doesn't say a word, which is worse than anything he could say. Nathaniel's breath catches; he is about to step out and go to his father. Alice puts her hand over his mouth. The candle goes, down the back stair toward the Works. She doesn't take her hand away fast enough, and neither of them mentions it. Crouched among Jamie's shoes, they find what they came for: a letter hidden in the boot-tree, an escape plan for two, and a diagram of a chain with a stair of thirteen steps pencilled beside it. At the window, going, there's a beat where neither of them knows what to say. Then she gives him one true thing: "Bounds." He asks for the rest of it. "Girls on Gin Lane don't give the rest of it to a Harding." So she's Bounds to him, and it's the only name he uses for her all season, first as a joke and then as something he's afraid to change. Below the window, Byron, passing with a candle, watches her drop into the garden, says nothing and smiles.
14. **The core.** Eldgard alone with the chained light, which we still only see as green glow and a voice. "He was mine, and I spent him." The chain has frayed links. He counts: "Forty-seven."
15. **Scotland Yard, then Gin Lane.** Felix has two frozen attendants, a missing corpse, papers that have already named the Frost Killer, and a Crown order on his desk telling him to drop the case, with Eldgard's influence all over it. He keeps the file and goes walking. On Gin Lane a witness saw a girl with frost hands on the Blackwood stair: "Bounds girl. Lives on Gin Lane with her sister." Felix knocks. Alice is out. The Lost One opens the door, frightened, and frost blooms. *Krrrk.* Felix's hand freezes to the doorframe, and she bolts.
16. **Tenement.** Alice comes home to a frozen door and Felix, his hand bandaged. "Your sister was dead. The surgeon said she was with child. Then she got up. Explain that to me." Alice: "Your surgeon was wrong. The cold fools them. They pull men out of the Thames in January who sit up on the slab." She believes every word, which is why Felix can't tell if she's lying. He doesn't arrest her. He files the word *child* away, and so does she, and neither of them says it again.
17. **Tyburn Road.** The Lost One, drawn by the Weeping, walks to the Harding Works and lays a mittened palm on the gate. Every lamp on the street bends its flame toward her. Nixon, on night watch, sees it.

**Tag**

18. **The core.** The chain jerks. For the first time in four hundred years the prisoner lifts her head, and we see her face: blue ice, green fire in the eyes. MORGANA: "My daughter is here." Eldgard drops his lamp.

## Episode 3 — "Lanterns"

**42 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** Nixon's hunt. **Set piece:** a leaper chase across the Christmas crystal market. **Sting:** the creature touches Nathaniel and he doesn't freeze, and she knows what he is.

*Logline:* Eldgard sets his captain on the Frost Killer, and Nathaniel, finally given a real job, arms the hunt without knowing its target is the girl Alice is hiding. When Nixon gets the creature at the end of his rifle, he has to choose between his patron's order and a frozen boy.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nixon | To bring in the Frost Killer quietly and repay Eldgard | Felix's questions, Cole's innocence, a creature that kills by touch | Leads the sweep personally | Chooses to save Cole and lets her go. Eldgard: "I know what you did at the core." Felix hands him Jamie's Newgate visitor slip |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **Charity school, Southwark, ten years ago.** Boys in a line. Eldgard pricks each boy's finger and touches the blood to a sliver of gallowglass. Most slivers stay dark. Thirteen-year-old Nixon's glows faintly green. Eldgard smiles and pats his head: "Still good." It plays as kindness, and it is something else (paid off in Episodes 7 and 9).

**Act One**

2. **Harding Works, the shop.** Eldgard gives Nathaniel his first real job: re-equip the Guard with leap-rigs and flash-gauntlets for the Frost Killer hunt. Nathaniel glows. Nixon is at the door for his own orders, and in the corridor, where his son can't hear, Eldgard gives them quietly: find the frost girl and bring her to the Works alive. Tell no one, not the Yard and not my son. Nixon: "Yes, sir." He owes, and now he's owned. *Therefore* the real son is arming the hunt, the spare is leading it, and neither of them knows the girl Alice is hiding is its target.
3. **Gin Lane, a pigeon loft.** Alice finds the Lost One on a rooftop having a serious conversation with pigeons. They write house rules in chalk: mittens on, no touching, cocoa allowed. Then the Lost One holds out an orange, peeled badly, in one long ruined spiral. "It was your birthday. On the bridge day. I forgot." A piece of Miriam's regret, surfacing in a creature who doesn't know why her hands wanted to do this. Alice stares at it. "You've never once remembered." "I know." Alice eats the whole thing, pith and all. The Lost One: "The light is crying. Can't you hear it?" Alice can't.
4. **Lambrick House, night.** Tamara opens her father's cipher safe in ninety seconds, holding a candle in her teeth. She copies the whole ledger page, not just the line: six months of payments to A.F., ending on the night Jamie died*.* On the stairs, AJAX FINK is eating an apple in the dark. "Evening, miss." He lets her pass.
5. **Guard tower roof.** Whimsy. Nixon trains Wicks on Nathaniel's new boosters. Cole fires his too early, sails off the roof into the laundry lines across the alley and dangles in someone's bloomers. Nixon laughs for the first time since the core, then remembers, and stops.

**Act Two**

6. **Vykra laundry.** Grise asks for Alice's report on the Harding son. Alice says he knows nothing. Grise: "Three days. Bring me something real, or the frost girl on Gin Lane becomes my business." *Therefore* Alice has to use Nathaniel.
7. **Harding Works, the shop.** Alice visits Nathaniel "to return the handkerchief" and lets him show off the boosters, which he does with puppyish joy: one jump across the foundry, *kshhh-THOOM*, and into the coal bin again, for an audience of one. Nobody has ever wanted so badly to be watched by her. She reads the sweep roster upside down while he's brushing off soot: Gin Lane, tonight. She feels sick at how easy it was, and sicker at how much she liked watching. She leaves without returning the handkerchief.
8. **Scotland Yard.** Felix, frostbitten hand in a sling, corners Nixon. The frost girl is Miriam Bounds, who was dead. Nixon thinks he's mad, until Felix adds that the Harding boy didn't die in a fall: "There was a bullet hole in his coat, Captain. Somebody burned the coat." He slides a Newgate visitor's slip across the desk: Jamie Harding, every Sunday for two years, prisoner 1417. "Someone ought to ask why." Both of Nixon's secrets are walking toward each other.
9. **The Serpentine, frozen pond.** Tamara takes Nathaniel skating, an engaged couple doing what's expected. Whimsy: he's terrible at it. She slips him the ledger copy. *A.F.* is Ajax Fink, paid on the night Jamie died. *Therefore* Nathaniel now has a suspect, and it's the wrong one.
10. **Christmas crystal market, dusk.** Alice moves the Lost One out of Gin Lane through the market crowds. The Lost One sees a hundred crystal lanterns and, enchanted, reaches out. Every crystal in the market flares at once. A Lantern lookout sees it.

**Act Three**

11. **The crystal market, set piece.** Nixon's leapers launch, *kshhh-THOOM, kshhh-THOOM*, over the stalls. Flash-bursts strobe the crowd. Alice runs the roofs on her ghostlight, and it coughs and sputters. Below, trapped and terrified, the Lost One screams. Every crystal in the market shatters, glass rains on the crowd, and a chestnut-seller freezes mid-shout. Four dead. At first light Felix walks through the glass and counts them, one by one.
12. **Bell tower, set piece.** Cole corners her alone. She reaches for him, *please*, and he freezes from the hand up. Nixon lands and fires a flash; she drops, stunned. Cole is turning blue. Nixon can take the girl to Eldgard, or carry Cole to the furnace room before his heart stops. He takes Cole. Alice drags the Lost One away.
13. **Harding House, morning.** The papers scream FOUR DEAD AT CRYSTAL MARKET. The servants say the Frost Killer was helped by a dark-haired girl with a ghostlight rig. Nathaniel remembers a hairpin and a gauntlet fixed in four seconds. Byron, pouring tea: "You're your mother's son, sir. You notice."
14. **Under the Works, an old lamp-tunnel.** Alice hides the Lost One where nobody would look: right under the Hardings. Four people are dead in the market. Alice, holding her with gloves on, says the thing you say: "You didn't mean it." It's the wrong sentence. It's what Miriam used to whisper under the bed about *him*, and it unlocks something in the body. Something runs down the Lost One's face and freezes. *Tink.* A bead of ice on the stone. Another. She catches one and stares at it. "What is this?" Alice, very quietly: "You're crying, Mim. You always cried. Under the bed, you cried for both of us." And the Lost One is suddenly full of a memory she has never lived: a buckle, a small girl's back, her own body not moving. "Ali. The belt. I should have—" Alice goes hard as iron. "We don't talk about him. That's behind the fence." The Lost One doesn't know what the fence is. She knows she has just been shut out of something that hurts, and that she wants in, to make it stop hurting. She remembers a woman in chains crying, too, and says nothing about that one. Alice holds her until the ice stops.
15. **Guy's Hospital.** Cole lives but loses two fingers. "Did I do good, sir?" "You did good." Eldgard is in the doorway. In the corridor: "You had her and you let her go." Then, softly: "I know what you did at the core, Captain. So do you."
16. **The tunnel mouth.** Nathaniel is waiting. The ghostlight parts are his own designs, stolen from his shop. Alice tells him the truth as she believes it: her sister nearly froze to death and came back wrong, and she's hiding her until she gets better. The Lost One steps out and, before Alice can stop her, takes Nathaniel's bare hand. Nothing happens. No frost. All three of them stare.
17. **Tamara's bedroom.** Ajax is sitting on her bed. "Your father sends his love. Stay out of his safe." He leaves the apple core on her pillow.

**Tag**

18. **The tunnel mouth.** Nathaniel turns to go. The Lost One, sniffing the air like an animal: "You smell like the man who keeps my mother." Cut to black.

## Episode 4 — "The Hearing"

**38 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** the Crown's hearing on the Harding Charter, and who killed Jamie. **Set pieces:** the coal-docks fight with Ajax; a murder on the witness stand. **Double twist:** the man Nathaniel hunts was hired by his own brother, and the "Frost Killer" who kills Felix in public is Nathaniel's mother.

*Logline:* Nathaniel and Alice hang Lambrick's enforcer over the Thames for Jamie's murder and learn Jamie paid him. Then Felix Hawke dies on the witness stand with frost on his lips, and all of London blames the girl hiding in the Harding glasshouse. Nixon, alone, finds the madman in Newgate whom Jamie visited every Sunday, and leaves with a nursery rhyme.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nathaniel | Jamie's killer | Ajax's Black Gauntlet; his father's half-truths | Ambushes Ajax with Alice | "Your brother paid me." The trail points back into his own house |
| Alice | To keep her place in the Vykra and keep the Lost One hidden | Grise orders her to hand the girl over | Refuses | Expelled. Loses the only family she had besides her sister |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **The Works gate, 2 November, ten minutes before the blackout.** Jamie meets Ajax and pays him two hundred guineas to cut the outer grid. In a coach, Lambrick: "Free her, and the light dies, and London comes back to coal. To me." Jamie: "Then London can freeze honestly." The audience now knows what Nathaniel doesn't: Jamie had partners.

**Act One**

2. **Harding glasshouse.** Nathaniel moves Alice and the Lost One into the abandoned orangery on the estate, the last place his father would look. Whimsy: frozen butterflies on the panes, an orange tree. The Lost One makes snow for the first time, a little flurry over her palm, and laughs like a child. Alice and Nathaniel watch her and have their first real conversation, about everything except the thing between them. Alice gives him her theory, the one she's built to live inside: "Something came back in her from the cold. It's got its own wants. But Mim's under it. Wren says the memories come back." Nathaniel has seen the girl touch his hand without freezing it. He looks at Alice with something like pity and doesn't argue. Later, the Lost One tries again, very carefully: "Ali. Under the bed—" Alice hands her an orange. "Eat that." Behind the fence.
3. **Harding House, breakfast.** Eldgard: the Crown's Charter hearing is in three days, and Nathaniel will sit beside him as heir. Josephine: the Lambrick wedding moves up. Nathaniel: "No." His first open defiance. Josephine reads the hearing's witness list and stops at one name: *Insp. F. Hawke.*
4. **Guard tower, the armoury.** A Crown order arrives from Vane after the market deaths: the Frost Killer is to be shot on sight. Cole is back, bandaged, two fingers short, desperate to be useful, and Nixon puts him on the duty roster to give him back his pride. Then Tamara comes to Nixon, the only man in London she trusts. Ajax was in her room, and her father paid Ajax the night Jamie died. She stands close. Nixon wants to say three things and says none of them. "I'll put a man on your door, miss." "I'd rather you were the man." Silence.
5. **Glasshouse workbench.** Alice's Vykra intelligence: Ajax collects from the coal docks on Thursday night. Nathaniel builds her a real gauntlet and fits it to her hand strap by strap. "Hold still, Bounds." She does, which she has never done for anyone. A love scene with no kissing in it.

**Act Two**

6. **Vykra laundry.** Grise: "Bring me the frost girl." Alice: "No." Grise cuts the Vykra knot out of Alice's glove with one snip. "Then you're no one's." Alice walks out into the rain with nowhere to belong. Under the awning, she takes his handkerchief out of her coat and folds it into the glove, where the knot used to be.
7. **Coal docks, Thursday night, set piece.** Nathaniel and Alice ambush Ajax among the coal barges. The Black Gauntlet hits like a steam hammer, *WHUNK*, and Nathaniel recognises his own stolen prototype in it. He's losing. Alice blinds Ajax with a flash-burst from her new gauntlet, and together they hook him onto a crane and swing him out over the black river.
8. **The crane.** Ajax, upside down, laughs. "Your brother paid me. Lambrick paid me too, but your brother paid double. I cut the outer grid. I never touched him." Then: "Your father went down after. Ask the Guard who fired." Alice: "Pull him up. He's the only witness we have." Nathaniel's hand is on the lever, and two true things pull at it: the man who helped kill his brother, or the one voice that can put Lambrick in the dock. "He's Jamie's grave," he says, and lets go. Ajax drops into the Thames. *Splash.* The Thames keeps him, for now.

It's half true, which is how Eldgard lies. Then the bait Nathaniel has wanted all his life: "After the hearing I'll take you into the work. All of it." On the back stairs afterwards, Josephine asks Byron where Nathaniel goes at night. Byron lies for the boy. It's the first time he has ever lied to her.
10. **Newgate, cell 1417.** Nixon, alone, in civilian coat, with the slip. The prisoner is an old man, TOBIAS MARSH, seventy if a day, in the dark at the end of the debtors' wing, picking at a string he calls his harp. Nixon: "Jamie Harding came here every Sunday. Why?" The old man hums, then pinches Nixon's cheek through the bars and cackles. "Marian's chin! Marian's chin!" Nixon goes still. "What did you say?" "Seven in the Orchard, ripe for the fall. One on the tree for the lantern's call. Thirteen steps down where the cold water drips, count the apples, kiss the stones' lips." Nixon grabs the bars: "Enough. Why was Jamie Harding visiting you? Tell me, you old fool." Marsh only cocks his head and smiles. "He cried too, you know. Same place in the song." Then he turns his back and goes back to his harp. Nixon leaves with a nursery rhyme and a cold feeling he can't name.
11. **Glasshouse.** Felix tracks Alice there. The Lost One offers him an orange and apologises for his hand, because she remembers. He sees a frightened girl. He was already listed to testify about the bullet hole in Jamie's coat. Now he'll say more: "Someone made her, and I'll say so in front of the Crown." *Therefore* he's now the most dangerous man in London.

**Act Three**

12. **Parliament, committee room, the hearing.** Lambrick argues that the Hardings are hiding something under the Works. Eldgard is calm. Vane presides. Nathaniel sits beside his father. Tamara is in the gallery, Nixon's Lanterns are at the doors, and a veiled woman sits in the back row. At the recess she crosses to the stand with a glass of water: "You look pale, Inspector." Felix drinks.
13. **The witness stand, set piece.** Felix: "The deaths at the market were caused by a girl who should be dead, and the Harding Works—" He coughs. Frost spreads across his lips and his eyes go white. He dies on the stand. The cry goes up: "The Frost Killer is here!" Doors slam. Nixon's Lanterns fire flash-bursts into the ceiling to stop the stampede. Vane orders the Frost Killer hunted "with every lamp in London." The vote is postponed, and sympathy swings toward the Hardings. In the crush on the gallery stairs, Tamara sees the veiled woman's glove, with a Vykra knot stitched inside it. A needle drops on the stair. Tamara pockets it.
14. **Glasshouse.** Nathaniel brings the news: Felix was killed by frost. Alice looks at the Lost One, who has been here with her all day. She knows the poison. "Winter's kiss. That's Vykra." *Therefore* somebody is framing her sister.
15. **Guard tower, the roof, then the armoury.** Nathaniel tells Nixon what Ajax said: ask the Guard who fired. Nixon goes very still. "Find me the name, Nix." "I'll find it." That night he finds it: his own rifle in the discharge log, one round, 2 November. He tears out the page and burns it in the stove. From the bunk-room door, Cole watches him do it, and later copies the page from memory into his diary.
16. **Vykra laundry.** Tamara puts the needle on Grise's counting table. "One of yours killed a policeman in front of the Crown. I want to know who. And I want in." Grise looks at her for a long time, then smiles. Alice was cut out yesterday, and Tamara is the new girl.

**Tag**

17. **Harding House, Josephine's dressing room.** Josephine lifts her veil, peels off gloves with a Vykra knot inside, and washes frost-dust from her fingertips in a porcelain bowl. The water skins over with ice. In the mirror, Byron is standing in the doorway.

## Episode 5 — "Lantern Night"

**33 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** The midseason turn. **Focus:** the heist into the core. **Set piece:** four separate plans on one festival night, ending with every lamp in London blazing white and bursting at once, and a crush on Westminster Bridge. (The city goes fully dark twice in this season: for an hour in the pilot, and for good in the finale. Lantern Night is the other thing light can do: it screams.) **Sting:** the light is a woman, the Tithe is Nathaniel, and the door is locked by his best friend.

*Logline:* On the night London celebrates its light, Nathaniel breaks into his own family's Works to see what Jamie died for. He finds a chained mother bleeding green, learns he's the next Harding meant to die for her, and hears the door lock behind him, on Nixon's order.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nathaniel | The truth about the core | His father, his best friend, his own hope that his father is good | Byron's key, Tamara's code, Alice's hands | He's the next Tithe, and he chooses to stay with his father anyway |
| Alice | To get her sister what she needs, and to keep her | The Lost One has her own plan | Goes in beside her | Morgana: "You have my daughter's hand. Give it back." |
| The Lost One | To free her mother tonight | The chain burns her, and Alice holds her back | Freezes the whole core | Her mother's pain bursts every lamp in London, people die in the crush, and she doesn't stop wanting it |
| Nixon | To obey the man who made him | The order is to trap his best friend | Warns Nathaniel with their boyhood whistle, then seals the door | Nathaniel sees his face through the glass |
| Tamara | To prove her mind is worth something | Alice's contempt, and locks that reset mid-heist | Recodes the inner door by ear | Becomes Alice's equal, and her rival |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **Tyburn, the same winter, 1489.** The Hargrave raid. Torches, young cousins of the Harding blood, and leading them the girl of nineteen from the treeline in the pilot, reaching the iron chain with a hammer. Alistair cuts her down at the lock. By dawn the empty gallows field from the pilot is full: three thousand Hargraves on Harding timber, and their wives and children walked down thirteen steps into the dark. The heist we're about to watch has been tried before, and it failed.

**Act One**

2. **Harding Works, the Upper Gallery.** Eldgard brings Nathaniel into "the work" at last: a cathedral of glass vats where engineers "grow gallowglass from Tyburn iron." Nathaniel's joy is real and painful to watch. "At the solstice I'll show you the rest."
3. **Glasshouse.** Nathaniel: "There's nothing down there but vats." The Lost One: "He showed you a nice room. My mother isn't in a nice room." Nathaniel defends his father; Alice defends her sister. "He's my father, Bounds." "She's my sister, Harding." Their first fight, and it's about family.
4. **Harding House, the silver pantry.** Byron gives Nathaniel a key on a black ribbon. Jamie left it with him. Byron guarded the core thirty years ago: "I heard her every night. I stopped hearing it when I stopped letting myself." He keeps quiet about Josephine's hands. Not yet.
5. **Vykra laundry.** Grise tests her new girl: a Works lock cipher, solved in four minutes. Grise lets slip that the inner codes change on Lantern Night. Tamara is welcomed with tea and a knife. For the first time in her life she belongs somewhere because of her mind.
6. **Guard tower.** Eldgard gives Nixon Lantern Night security for the Works, plus a private order. "My son will come tonight. Let him through. Seal the core behind him. The girl doesn't leave." Nixon: "And Nathaniel?" "He's my son, Captain. He'll be with me."
7. **Glasshouse, planning.** Whimsy. A model of the Works built out of teacups and oranges; the Lost One eats the core. Tamara arrives with the code, invited by Nathaniel. Alice and Tamara meet: the fiancée and the undercity girl, the new Vykra and the cut one. Every line is polite and every line is a knife. Nathaniel is the only one who doesn't notice.

**Act Two**

8. **Lantern Night, the Strand.** Fireworks over a blazing green city, crowds, the Lord Mayor. In the crowd, Lambrick and a scarred, very alive Ajax with a coal-mob. Their plan: cut the outer grid during the finale and let London see Harding light fail before the vote.
9. **Thames outfall, the heist begins.** The pipe is full of black water. The Lost One freezes it into a glass road and they walk inside the river. Jamie's key opens the first grate.
10. **The gantries.** Nixon, on the high walk, sees them pass. Orders say let them through. He gives the whistle they invented at nine years old, *turn back*. Nathaniel hears it, smiles because it's Nix, and keeps going.
11. **The Strand, finale.** Ajax's crew cuts the outer grid. The East End goes dark in the middle of the fireworks. Inside the Works, every lock resets, and the heisters are trapped between two doors with the Guard alarm ringing.
12. **The shaft.** Tamara recodes the inner door by ear, listening to the tumblers, while Alice holds her by the belt over a crystal shaft four hundred feet deep. Neither of them says thank you. *Click.* It opens.
13. **The core.** They go down, and we see her whole for the first time. MORGANA: nine feet of ice and green fire, iron hooks through her shoulders and hips, her blood running green along channels into London. She is singing, and it's the Weeping: the Lantern Hymn's tune, with none of the words.

**Act Three**

14. **The chain.** "Little frost. You came." The Lost One grabs the chain, and it burns her mittens to smoke. Alice pulls her back. Morgana, to Alice: "You have my daughter's hand. Give it back." Alice, holding tighter: "She's my sister. Whatever came back in her, my sister's under it." Morgana tilts her great head and looks at Alice with something terribly like pity. She doesn't correct her. The Lost One doesn't either. Then, to Nathaniel, almost gently: "You came down these stairs once, little Harding. And your brother is not dead. He is cold."
15. **The lock.** Nathaniel touches the chain, and it tightens. Morgana screams, and across London the lamps surge brighter. His blood feeds it. Jamie's glass blade is still stuck in the lock where he left it, and his diagram suddenly makes sense.
16. **The core.** Eldgard steps out of the dark; he's been waiting. "Now you know what your brother knew." The Tithe: every fifty winters, at first snow, a Harding gives his life to the chain, or it breaks and London freezes. "I raised Jamie for it. Jamie ran. Now it's you." Nathaniel: "Why not you?" Eldgard doesn't answer. The silence goes on a beat too long, and Nathaniel files it away.
17. **The core door.** It seals with a hiss, iron on glass. Behind the glass is Nixon, following orders. Nathaniel: "Nix!" Nixon doesn't look away, and that's worse.
18. **The core, escape.** The Lost One, desperate, lets go of the cold. The core freezes around her mother, and Morgana screams, the way she screamed when Nathaniel touched the chain, only worse. Her blood flares through every channel at once. Across London, on Lantern Night, every lamp blazes white for one heartbeat and bursts. *Pop-pop-pop-pop*, street after street, a wave of shattering glass under the fireworks. The Lost One flinches as if she heard every one. Alice blows the door with her gauntlet. Tamara drags the Lost One into the outfall. Alice reaches for Nathaniel. He stays: "I need to hear the rest." She leaves him there.
19. **Westminster Bridge.** Glass rains on the festival crowd from the bridge lamps, then only fireworks for light, then a panicking crowd and a crush. Cole's squad hold a barricade with flash-bursts; people still die against the railings. Lambrick, on a coal cart: "Remember who kept you warm!"
20. **The core, after.** Father and son, alone with the prisoner. Nathaniel: "She said Jamie isn't dead. She said he's cold." Eldgard doesn't look at Morgana when he answers. "She tells every Harding whatever will bring him to the lock. She told me my sister was still singing." He says it like a man pressing on an old scar. "Thirty-three days. I won't lose two sons. Help me find another way." Nathaniel can't tell if any of it is a lie. He says yes. He chooses his father, and he puts the word *cold* away where he keeps the weeping in the floor.

**Tag**

21. **Gin Lane rooftop, the black city.** Alice and the Lost One, soaked and shaking. The Lost One: "I'm going to free her. Even if all of them freeze." Alice takes her burned hand anyway. Below them, London is lit in patches, every third lamp a broken stump.

## Episode 6 — "Coal"

**28 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** a city in the cold, and Byron. **Set piece:** the Pimlico gasworks explosion, five plans meeting in one tunnel. **Sting:** Byron dies before he can say what he came to say, and then he says it.

*Logline:* With Harding light failing and Lambrick coal filling the streets, Alice and the Lost One try to reach the core through the gas tunnels on the night of the Charter vote. Ajax has rigged those tunnels, Nixon is waiting in them, Nathaniel runs in after them, and Byron runs in after him.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nathaniel | To find "another way" with his father: the Heartglass | He doesn't know what the Heartglass needs | Builds it anyway, then runs to warn Alice | Byron dies pulling him out, and tells him who killed Felix |
| Alice | To get the Lost One to her mother before she forgets everything | Tamara's warning: the tunnels are rigged | Goes anyway | The Lost One saves four children and forgets Alice's name |
| Tamara | To save Nathaniel, and herself | Her father plans to blow the Works' conduit during the vote | Warns Nathaniel in their childhood cipher | Her warning is what brings Nathaniel, and so Byron, into the blast |
| Nixon | To take the girl alive, and buy time with Cole | Cole's ultimatum, Alice's gauntlet | Ambushes in the tunnel | Carries Cole out of the fire, and owes him everything |
| Josephine | To keep Byron silent without killing him | Thirty years of love | Lets him live | Sends Eldgard the tip that sends Byron to his death |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **The core gate, thirty years ago.** Young BYRON, a Lantern sergeant on night watch, catches a Vykra girl with a knife sent to kill young Eldgard. It's JOSEPHINE, nineteen. He lets her go. A year later she marries the man she was sent to kill. Byron serves at the wedding. In his tunic pocket is a faded blue ribbon, from a different night, fifty years gone.

**Act One**

2. **London, the cold.** Since Lantern Night half the lamps in London are broken glass, and the rest come and go. Coal carts with LAMBRICK on the side. Prices chalked up: doubled, then tripled. A child asleep in a doorway with frost in her eyelashes, and a constable looking away. Grim and quiet.
3. **Harding Works.** Eldgard and Nathaniel design the **Heartglass**, a resonance cage meant to hold the chain without a Harding death. Father and son at one bench, finishing each other's equations. It's everything Nathaniel ever wanted. Eldgard: "We'll need something of her blood to tune it." Nathaniel assumes he means Morgana.
4. **Gin Lane, a borrowed room.** Wren said the memories would come back. They're going the other way. Every use of the cold costs one of Miriam's memories, and tonight the Lost One asks, "When's your birthday, Ali?" The same day Miriam forgot it, and the same day the Lost One remembered it with an orange. Alice: "You knew it once." The Lost One, frightened, grabbing Alice's sleeve: "There's something I have to say to you before it goes. About him. About the belt." Alice: "After." "There might not be an after." "*After*, Mim." The fence holds, and the Lost One watches the thing she needs to say slipping further away. Then, harder, because she's frightened: "If I free my mother, I'll be strong again." Alice: "If you free her, the light goes out and people die." The Lost One: "People die now. I watched one on your step."
5. **Harding Works, the shop window, midnight.** Alice climbs the Works wall to tell the only person who would understand that her sister has forgotten her birthday. Through the lit glass she sees Nathaniel and Eldgard still at one bench, finishing each other's equations, his father's hand on his shoulder, the Heartglass half-built between them. It's everything he has wanted his whole life, and she can see that it is. She doesn't knock. Her breath fogs the pane. She wipes it clean with her glove, the one with his handkerchief folded inside it, and goes back down the wall. A second later Nathaniel looks up, at a window with nobody in it.
6. **Lambrick House, the gun room.** Tamara, behind a curtain, hears her father and Ajax: charges along the Tyburn conduit where it meets his Pimlico gas main, set to blow on the night of the vote so Harding's Works look rotten to the Crown. Ajax: "And if your daughter gets in the way?" Lambrick, after a pause: "Then my daughter should have stayed home."

**Act Two**

7. **A Covent Garden tea shop.** Alice swallows her pride and asks Tamara, the new Vykra with a coal baron's maps, for a way into the core. Tamara tells her about the charges. The only route in runs through Ajax's tunnels. Tamara: "Then go another night." Alice: "There isn't one. She's forgetting me." Two girls who dislike each other, starting to understand each other. The girl who pours their tea has a knot stitched inside her glove, and neither of them looks at her hands.
8. **The frozen canal at Little Venice.** They meet in secret under a bridge, snowless cold, their breath smoking between them. He tells her about the Heartglass: "We can save both of them." She tells him the Lost One is forgetting her. When she pulls off a glove to warm her hands, something falls out of it into the frost: his handkerchief, folded small, kept where a Vykra keeps the thing that says whose she is. He picks it up. "You kept it." "Bounds keeps things." For once in his life he doesn't wait to be reached for. He kisses her, and finds out she has been waiting since the ballroom. *Tik. Tik.* Ice ticking off the bridge above them. Then both of them use the kiss to hide the dangerous part: she's going in tonight, and he still doesn't know what the Heartglass is for.
9. **Harding House, the linen room, then the study.** Byron confronts Josephine. "I saw your hands." "I did it for Nathaniel." "You always say that." She has a needle in her sleeve, and she doesn't use it. "Thirty years, Josie." He leaves. She sits on the linen and doesn't cry. Then a maid brings her a folded note in Grise's hand, bought an hour ago from a tea-girl in Covent Garden: *Tyburn conduit. Vote night. The girl.* Josephine takes it straight down to her husband's study. "How do you know?" "I'm your wife." Eldgard sends for Nixon: take the girl alive. The Heartglass needs her. The woman who has just spared Byron's life has set the trap he will die in, and neither of them knows it.
10. **Harding Works, then the Harding House hall.** A cipher note from Tamara in their childhood code reaches Nathaniel: *The conduit. Charges. She's going in.* He runs, grabbing the leap-gauntlet as he goes. In the hall, Byron sees him tear out of the yard. He takes his old Lantern from its glass case on the wall, still charged after thirty years, and follows.

**Act Three**

11. **The Tyburn conduit, set piece.** Alice and the Lost One go down. Ajax's men are lighting fuses in a side drain. Four tunnel children are sleeping in the warm pipe-nook beyond it. Nixon's squad waits in the dark ahead, Cole among them. In the dark, Cole holds up his diary: "Tell Mr. Harding, sir, or I will." Nixon: "After the vote." Cole agrees, because he still believes in him.
12. **The ambush.** Flash-bursts. Nixon corners the Lost One, and she grabs his bare wrist. No frost. He doesn't freeze, the way Nathaniel didn't, and in the strobe and the shouting nobody sees it but the camera. She goes white anyway, because she can smell it on him the way she smelled it on Nathaniel: "They told you your mother had a fever. She didn't." He lets go as if burned. Alice fights Nixon, gauntlet to gauntlet, in a tunnel too narrow to swing in: the two leads' first real clash. Neither of them wants to win. Then Nathaniel arrives screaming "Gas!" Ajax, seeing the Guard, lights early. The Lost One's cold meets a flash-burst in the main.
13. **Pimlico, then Parliament.** The gasworks erupts. *WHOOOM.* Fire rolls along the river. A mile downriver the Charter vote is three names from done: Eldgard, Lambrick and Vane in their places, Nathaniel's chair empty, and Josephine looking at it when the windows blow in on the voting lords.
14. **The rubble.** Byron lifts the beam off Nathaniel and takes its weight himself, and it crushes him. Dying, he says: "Your mother killed the inspector. She does it for you. Don't let her." Then: "You were always enough." He dies with his hand on the boy's face. In his other fist is the blue ribbon.
15. **The river stair.** Nixon carries Cole out of the fire. Alice comes up with the Lost One, who uses the last of her cold to smother the flames around four trapped children. Then she looks at Alice politely, like a stranger. "Thank you, miss." She doesn't know her name. Alice tells herself it's the shock again, worse this time. It's the last time she'll manage to.
16. **Parliament, broken glass.** Vane, bleeding from the temple, announces that the Charter is suspended and the Works pass to a Crown board. Lambrick takes a seat on it. Eldgard has lost the Works.

**Tag**

17. **Harding House, the music room.** Nathaniel walks in covered in ash. Josephine is at the piano. "Byron's dead." She weeps, really. "He told me about Inspector Hawke." The music stops. A long beat. "Then you know I love you."

## Episode 7 — "The Girl Who Isn't"

**21 DAYS TO FIRST SNOW.** **Focus:** two reveals on one day: the sister and the best friend. **Set piece:** wolves of the White run through Whitechapel, and Nathaniel and Nixon fight side by side an hour after the friendship breaks. **Sting:** Alice chooses the creature anyway, and Nixon learns he was raised as spare parts.

*Logline:* Alice finds the letter Miriam never left her and learns that the thing she has been protecting is not her sister. Nixon finally tells Nathaniel who fired the shot. Then the chain thins enough for the White to come through, and the only way through the night is together.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alice | The truth about who she's been protecting | The truth: Miriam was leaving her, and this isn't Miriam | Throws the Lost One out | Runs into a wolf attack for her, and chooses her: "I'm Alice. I'm your sister." |
| The Lost One | To be wanted for herself | She has lost Alice's name, Alice has thrown her out, and Miriam's love for Jamie keeps surfacing in her body | Goes to the place she first came through | Commands the wolves in front of the city, and becomes the Frost Witch |
| Nixon | To put the lie down | Nathaniel's love for him | Confesses at their boyhood spot | Learns from Eldgard's own file that he was bred as a reserve |
| Nathaniel | To know what his father did, and what he made | Every adult he loves is lying | Opens Jamie's coffin himself, then corners Eldgard with a fistful of slag | His brother's coffin holds no body, and the Heartglass is a cage for the Lost One |
| Tamara | To stop her father, and to stop being polite | A wedding in seven days | Takes the seized Harding papers and gives Nixon his file | Kisses Nixon a week before she marries his best friend |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **Gin Lane, 2 November, morning.** Miriam's last day, from Miriam's side. She is radiant, sick in a basin, and hand on her belly. Alice is asleep on the other side of the bed. Miriam starts a letter: *Ali, about him, when we were small—* She stares at it a long time. She scores it out until the paper tears. She starts again: *I'm leaving with him. I'm sorry, Ali. Don't follow me.* She means to leave it on Alice's pillow, loses her nerve, and sews it into her coat lining. Then she pulls on the red mittens.

**Act One**

2. **Gin Lane, a borrowed room.** Afternoon. Alice mends the Lost One's coat, burned in the gasworks, and her needle catches on something in the lining. Folded paper, Miriam's hand. She reads it twice. *I'm leaving with him. I'm sorry, Ali. Don't follow me.* Miriam was leaving her and never meant to say goodbye. Then the door bangs. The Lost One, home from the market with an armful of oranges she is enormously proud of, chattering about a man who sells hot chestnuts out of a drum. She doesn't use Alice's name; she hasn't since the gasworks. Alice puts the letter in her apron pocket, smiles, and peels her an orange. She says nothing. She says nothing all evening, and we watch her not say it.
3. **The same room. The peak of the sisters' story.** Late, one candle. Alice holds out the letter. "You were leaving." The Lost One, who lost Alice's name in the gasworks, still knows this face and still knows this hurt. "Yes." And the fence comes down, because Alice is too angry to hold it up: "You always left. You left me under that bed. Every time. You promised nothing would touch me, and then you counted fence posts while he—" She can't finish. Fifteen years, and she has finally said it out loud, to the only person who was there.

    The Lost One has been waiting six weeks to be let in. She has the guilt, and it's the last thing of Miriam's left in her, because the cold took the birthdays and the songs and the name and couldn't get its teeth into this. She kneels on the floor so she's lower than Alice, the way you'd kneel to a child under a bed. "I should have stood in front of you. Every time. I'm sorry. I was so frightened of him that I let you be frightened alone, and I've hated myself every day since." Then the thing Miriam never could have said: "If he came through that door now, I'd stand in front of you. I'd let him freeze."

    It is the apology Alice has wanted her whole life, word for word, and it's perfect. That's how she knows. Miriam couldn't have said it. Miriam *flinched*. Alice, barely a breath: "Say my name." The Lost One opens her mouth, and there's nothing there. A long silence. "You're not her."

    "No." Plainly: Morgana's daughter, who came through on the bridge, wearing Miriam's body and Miriam's memories like a coat. Alice: "Where is she?" "Gone. I'm sorry. She was going to leave you, Ali. She told me on the bridge. She was frightened, and it wasn't of you." Then the real horror, said gently, as a gift: "I'm not sorry for her. I'm sorry for me. I love you. I don't know whose that is anymore. I think it's mine."

    Alice looks at the thing that has been her sister for six weeks, the one that remembered her birthday, peeled her an orange and just gave her the only apology she has ever been given, and understands that her real sister never loved her this well. "Get out." The door closes on her.
4. **Harding House, breakfast.** Eldgard sits silent and diminished without his Works. Josephine runs the table now: the wedding is in seven days, and the Lambrick money will buy the Works back. Nathaniel: "Or what, Mother? You'll poison me?" Josephine, buttering toast: "If I had to choose between you and London, I chose a long time ago."
5. **The core, under Crown control.** Vane and Lambrick tour it with Tamara on her father's arm. On the stair down, Lambrick, quietly, the only time he talks to her as if she were a person: "Your mother hated the green. Said it made everyone at dinner look drowned. I kept her room on coal till the end." Then he looks up at Morgana. There's no horror in his face, only arithmetic: "She could be bled faster." The man who kept one room warm out of love would freeze a city for the same reason, and calls both of them providing. On the clerk's desk sits Eldgard's seized Heartglass folio. Tamara reads one line: *Vessel: White-born.*

**Act Two**

6. **Blackwood Bridge, fog.** The Lost One, thrown out, walks to the place she first came through. Howls in the fog. Wolves of the White, white-furred and blue-eyed, pad out of nothing and bow to her. That's the most dangerous thing that could happen.
7. **Guard tower roof, their boyhood spot.** This morning Nixon got a medal for carrying Cole out of the gasworks, and the ceremony was pure irony. Afterwards Cole, two fingers short, held out the diary: "Today, sir." So this afternoon, at the spot they've had since they were nine, Nixon tells him. "I fired the shot. I didn't know it was him." Nathaniel hits him once, hard. Nixon lets him. "Your father told me to lie. He came down after." "Was Jamie alive when you left?" "Yes." Nathaniel walks away without a word. A friendship of fifteen years ends on a roof.
8. **A Gin Lane gin shop.** Tamara finds Alice drunk. She tells her about Lambrick's arithmetic and the line in the folio. Alice: "Why would I care? She's not my sister." Tamara: "She put out a fire around four children with the last of her cold and forgot your name doing it. Did Miriam ever do that?" Alice, after a long time: "No. Miriam never stood in front of anybody." She stops drinking.
9. **Whitechapel, set piece.** Where the chain is thinnest, over the old Hargrave plague pits, where the hanged were dumped, the fog splits. Wolves pour into the gaslit streets. Gas lamps freeze and burst, *tink, tink, tink*. Bodies in the snowless street.
10. **Commercial Road, set piece.** Nixon's Lanterns respond: leapers on the rooftops, flash-bursts (the wolves scatter from the light), quench-blades. Shot the way the pilot's core raid was: black, then a strobe frame, then black. Every flash shows us a wolf somewhere new and closer. We see them only in the frames the Lanterns light, which is scarier than a wide shot and costs a tenth as much. Nixon fights like a man trying to get himself killed. Cole fights beside him with his two-finger grip.
11. **The Pavilion music hall, set piece.** Whimsy into grim. We stay inside. A comic singer keeps a hundred people singing "Daisy Bell" to drown out the scratching at the doors, and the song gets louder every time the scratching does. A door panel bows inward. A child stops singing, and her mother starts her again. Nathaniel arrives on leap-boosters with a bag of flash-cartridges, and Nixon arrives from the other roof. No words. They fight back to back the way they did at nine years old, perfectly in sync, an hour after the friendship ended.
12. **The Pavilion stage door, and the street.** The Lost One walks into the street of wolves and says one word in a language of wind. They stop, every one of them, except the biggest. She kills it with cold, and loses another memory. A hundred people watch a girl command wolves. By morning she'll be the Frost Witch. Then Alice, sober now, reaches her through the street of wolves. The Lost One doesn't know her face anymore. Alice: "I'm Alice. I'm your sister." The sisters' scene: chosen, not inherited.

**Act Three**

13. **Whitehall, then Shadwell, dawn.** Two buyers for one girl. In his office, Vane declares martial law: the Frost Witch is to be captured. ## Episode 10 — "The Tithe"

**THE LONGEST NIGHT.** Midnight to dawn. **Focus:** the choice Alistair refused. **Set piece:** London goes dark for good, and the White comes through. **Double twist:** Nathaniel refuses every sacrifice offered to him, Morgana still demands a Harding, and Josephine goes in his place. **Final image:** "The Record," a montage of the season's climaxes under Nathaniel's voice, ending on a heartbeat, then a hard cut to Jamie, alive in the White.

*Logline:* The Heartglass cracks, the Crown comes for the last Harding son, and Nixon offers himself instead. Nathaniel does what no Harding has done in four hundred years. He opens the lock, and London pays in light.

| Character | Want | Obstacle | Solution | Twist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nathaniel | To end the pact without spending a life | The Crown, his mother, and a city that will freeze | Opens the lock with his own blood and his father's ring | Becomes the most hated man in London, and his mother pays the debt. He lets her, and refuses to let anyone call it fever |
| Alice | To keep her sister | Her sister's own mother | Lets her go | The Lost One stays because Alice let her go |
| The Lost One | To free her mother, and to have a sister | She can't have both | Stands between her mother and the city | Tells Alice what Miriam asked on the bridge, and stays, mortal and warm |
| Nixon | To mean something | The wolves, Ajax, losing Cole | Offers his life on purpose, then holds the bridges all night | Refused as a sacrifice; ends as captain, chosen by his own men |
| Tamara | To be the heir | Her father, and the vault | Offers him her hand; he chooses the crystals | Heir of Lambrick and wife of Harding: she ends the season holding both houses |
| Josephine | Her son alive | Morgana's price | Takes his place | Walks into the White, and finds who's waiting there |

### Scenes

**Cold open**

1. **The core, 1489, an hour before the pilot's cold open.** Alistair at the chain, his net still wet with frost; the child is in it. Morgana, fresh in her hooks, makes him an offer: "Free me, and pay in light. Keep me, and pay in sons." Then the part no Harding ever wrote down: "Either way, my child goes free tonight." He slides the wolf-crest ring onto his finger. "Sons." He cuts the net. In the doorway behind him, his firstborn, Edric, nine years old, the boy with the lantern from the pilot, holds it up for his father, then carries it down the stair ahead of him, alone. The same choice Nathaniel faces tonight.

**Act One**

2. **The core, midnight.** The lever drops. The Heartglass floods the Lost One with green fire, and she screams, and the door swings open for her mother: the hooks loosen, and for the first time in four hundred years Morgana could step out. She doesn't. She takes the chain in both hands and drags herself back onto the iron, hooks tearing deeper, pulling against the trade until the glass around her daughter cracks from her side. *Krrrrk.* "Not her." The chain shudders and doesn't take. At the lever, Josephine watches a mother refuse the door and hears her husband in Newgate: *You would.* Lambrick, from the gallery: "Then the boy! Take the Harding!" Vane's marshals start down the stairs.
3. **The core floor.** Alice smashes the Heartglass with her gauntlet, *KSSHHH*, and drags the Lost One out, half turned to crystal. Josephine draws her knives to put the girl back in the cracked glass, because the marshals are already on the stairs for her son and a broken cage is still a cage until midnight. She fights like a woman who has just been shown she's wrong, and they fight, Vykra against Vykra, the mother against the girl she paid to love her son. Josephine is better. Nathaniel steps between them: "Mother."
4. **The core floor.** Nixon comes down the stair bleeding, with Tamara behind him. "I'm Harding blood. Take me. They grew me for this the way they grew my mother. Let me be something on purpose." Cole: "Sir, no."
5. **The lock.** Nathaniel: "No one goes in the chain again." Jamie's glass blade is still in the lock where he left it on the first night, frozen into the iron by four hundred years of cold. Nathaniel pulls, and it doesn't move. *Krrk.* His hands slip on it. Alice is beside him before he asks, and the gauntlet he built for her in the glasshouse, strap by strap, is already whining up to overload, *eeeeeeee*, the way he told her never to run it. He knows what she's about to do. "If this works, every light in London goes out. They'll hate me for a hundred years." "Us," she says, and puts the gauntlet's core against the iron. The heat comes back through the plate into her palm and she holds it there, the smell of her own glove burning, until the ice round the blade lets go. He pulls it free, cuts his palm on it, and presses his blood and his father's wolf ring into the iron. His father's warning is in his ear: *hoping*. He can't swear his brother isn't part of why his hand is steady. Harding blood closed it, and Harding blood opens it, but it took an undercity girl's burned hand to get the blade out. Somewhere above, a church bell strikes twelve.
6. **London.** The chain falls. The last time London went dark, the light came back within the hour. This time the green dies street by street, the same wave as the pilot, and nothing comes back. One wordless shot after another: a tram dying in the snow, a hospital ward going black, a mother on Gin Lane lighting a tallow candle with shaking hands.

**Act Two**

7. **The core.** Morgana rises out of the pit as her wounds close, nine feet tall. She looks down at Nathaniel: "A Harding opened it." She doesn't thank him. The White pours through behind her, with wind, snow and wolves, and she goes up through the Works roof toward Tyburn hill.
8. **The Works vault.** Lambrick is stuffing the last gallowglass into satchels; it's the most valuable thing left on earth. Tamara finds him and offers her hand: "Come out, Father." He holds up a satchel to her, almost pleading: "This is for you. All of it. It was always for you." "Then leave it." He looks at the crystals. He looks at her. He can't put them down; he never could. She walks out. Behind her the wolves come into the vault.
9. **The core stair.** Nixon against Ajax: quench-blade against Black Gauntlet. Nixon takes the quick-takedown: blade into the gauntlet's crystal, which freezes the piston, then into Ajax. He dies frozen mid-laugh, his apple rolling down the stairs.
10. **London, the longest night.** Nixon's forty and every loyal Lantern form flash-lines across the bridges to keep the wolves off the tenements. The Vykra hold Gin Lane with knives and candles. Alice holds the lane with the Lost One wrapped in coats in a doorway behind her, and between wolves she grabs the tea-girl from Covent Garden, the one with the knot in her glove. "Go to the Works. Find out if he's alive. Don't tell him I asked." The girl goes. Alice is three steps after her before she knows she's moving; behind her a child starts to scream, *aaaAAH*, and she turns back to her knife. Every time a flash lights the sky over Tyburn, she looks. Tamara throws open every Lambrick coal depot free to the city, and there are fires in every street. Whimsy in the dark: Cole teaches a crowd of children to make flash-shadow rabbits on the walls.
11. **Blackfriars Bridge.** The charges run out along Cole's line. He holds it with his last cartridge, two fingers short and seventeen years old, until it's empty. Nixon finds him in the last dark before dawn, sitting up against the parapet as if he were resting, his diary open in his lap to the burned log page.
12. **The core, empty.** Nathaniel and Josephine alone, the hooks hanging empty. He tells her about the coffin: slag from the vats, no body, and the word *cold*. She wept over that box for a week, and her husband let her. "Dead is dead, Nathaniel. I've made enough dead to know." She doesn't believe it, and she says it anyway. Then: "I'd do it all again." "I know. That's why I can't be you." At the foot of the stair she stops. "I paid for that girl." "You paid for a week, Mother. You couldn't afford the rest." Josephine almost smiles. It's the first thing about her son she has ever failed to buy. Boots on the stair: the tea-girl, bleeding from the scalp. "Gin Lane's holding. Just. She said not to tell you she asked." Nathaniel is at the stair before she has finished. And Josephine, who has controlled every door in his life, says: "Go to her." It's so unlike her that he stops with his hand on the rail and looks at her, really looks, and sees a woman who has already decided something. "Not until you tell me where you're going." She won't. So he stays, and doesn't go to the girl he loves, because he's watching his mother the way she taught him to watch everyone. He's still watching when she starts up the hill.

**Act Three**

13. **Tyburn hill, set piece.** Morgana stands over London in the storm, with wolves in a ring around her. Four hundred years of grievance. Josephine climbs, and Nathaniel climbs after her. From the Gin Lane side, Alice comes up the hill carrying the Lost One. Across the snow they see each other for the first time since the lock, alive, and there's no time for any of it.
14. **Tyburn hill.** The Lost One sets herself down in front of Alice, between her mother and the city, the place Miriam never once stood. Alice sees it, and her breath goes. The Lost One sings the hymn back to her mother in the old tongue, the way it was before it had words. The wolves stop circling. "They let you go. Let them go." Morgana: "They bled me for four hundred years." "One of them let you go."
15. **Tyburn hill.** Morgana: "Then come home, little frost." The Lost One looks at Alice. Alice, who has spent the season holding on, lets go: "Go, if you want to. I'll be all right." She means it. She has done this once before, with a nod, at an altar.
16. **Tyburn hill.** The Lost One tells her the last thing: "On the bridge she was still alive. She asked me something. 'Go back to Ali. Stand in front of her. I never did.' I said yes. Then I wanted to." Miriam's last thought was Alice, after all, and her last act was to find someone braver to keep the promise she broke. Alice laughs and cries at once: her sister did love her. She just couldn't do it herself. The Lost One stays. Morgana, who tore her own hooks rather than let her daughter pay, lets her stay, which is harder. Then she names her price: "A Harding chained me. A Harding comes with me." Nathaniel steps forward, and Alice steps in front of him, into the place nobody ever stood for her, her fist in his coat. Her first word to him all night: "Don't."
17. **Tyburn hill.** Josephine steps past him and says to Morgana the words Morgana said at the lever: "Not him." Morgana looks at her for a long moment and knows her. Then, to her son: "I'm a Harding. I chose the name. I swore on that lead with my eyes shut." He catches his mother's wrist. Alice is still in front of him, her fist still in his coat. Two women holding him, one from each side of his life, and both of them are right. The wolves shift their weight in the snow. Josephine, quietly, the only time all season she asks him for anything: "They'll say I died of fever in Switzerland." Nathaniel: "No. They'll say your name." A long beat. His hand opens. "Go." She kisses his forehead. To Alice: "Keep him out of the Works." It's the contract she paid for in Episode 2, now given as a blessing. She takes Morgana's hand and they walk into the white. The wolves follow, and the storm goes with them.

    *Why this isn't Otty again:* Otty went willingly too, and his grandfather let her. The difference between Nathaniel and every Harding before him isn't the choice. It's the lie. Otty got a brass plate that said DIED ABROAD. Josephine gets the record (scenes 19 and 20).
18. **Tyburn hill, dawn.** The snow stops in the middle of falling. Nathaniel is on his knees where his mother was, and for a long time nothing happens. Alice doesn't touch him. She kneels in front of him in the snow and lets him have it, because nobody ever let her. When it comes it isn't crying. It's a sound with no word in it, *hhh, hhh*, a man trying to breathe around something too big for his chest. She stays where he can see her until it passes. Then she takes the handkerchief out of her glove, the one he gave her at his brother's grave, and tears it in two. *Rrrip.* Half goes round the palm he cut at the lock. He takes the other half out of her fingers without asking and binds the hand she burned for him. Neither of them is any good at it. She doesn't know what to say to a man who has just said "Go" to his mother, so she gives him the most private thing she owns. Under her breath, the way Miriam taught her under the bed, she counts for him. "One post. Two post." He knows it. He heard it in a ballroom corridor five weeks ago and never asked. He counts with her. At eight she stops, because eight is where it has always ended. He keeps going. "Nine." Nobody told him about nine. He got there on his own. And then, for the first time in ten episodes, he says her name. "Alice." She hears it. He has never once said it, and she knows which bill he read it on. He's saying it now, when nobody has paid for it. He kisses her, or she kisses him; for the rest of their lives they will disagree about which.
19. **Westminster, first light.** The first dawn of the episode, grey through the tall windows, coal smoke rising from Tamara's fires. Vane names Nathaniel "the man who put out London" and signs a warrant. Tamara, Lady Harding and heir to Lambrick, puts the Heartglass folio on his desk, with the Crown's seal on the binding order. Nixon lays Cole's diary beside it, taken from the dead boy's lap: the burned discharge-log page copied word for word, and under it, in a seventeen-year-old's careful hand, *2 Nov. Capt. hit him in the shoulder. Mr Harding sent the Capt. to the gate. The man on the floor was still talking.* It puts Nixon's shot on the record, and the man who stayed behind with a living boy. Nixon could burn that page a second time. He sets it down open. Vane slides a captain's lantern across the desk to Nixon, the price of his silence. Nixon leaves it there: "The Guard will keep its own flame." Vane, cornered, offers Nathaniel the pardon on one condition: the Crown seals what happened under Tyburn, for the public good. Nathaniel: "No. Write it down." Vane: "The Crown doesn't keep that kind of record." "Then start." Nathaniel turns to the clerk, and the clerk dips his pen. "Edric Harding—"

20. **The Record, final montage.** Nathaniel's dictation carries over everything that follows. It starts as testimony, read flat and exact like a ledger, and somewhere in the middle it stops being for the clerk. No score until line 6, then the Lantern Hymn, the tune only, the way Morgana sang it. Every image is a climax from the season, seen the morning after.

**Final scene**

21. **The White.** Hard cut on the heartbeat. Wind, and nothing else. Endless snow. Josephine walks beside Morgana through the drifts, where the chain's dead went, and stops. A man is standing in the snow with frost in his hair and his breath smoking. He's breathing. JAMIE HARDING turns, and he doesn't understand what he's seeing. "Mother?" Josephine's face: four hundred years of Harding grief, and for one second, joy. Far behind him, small in the white, a boy of nine holds up a lantern that has been burning for four hundred years. Cut to black.

    The card that opened every episode comes up one last time. It doesn't count down. **DAY ONE.**

**What Season 2 inherits**

- **A London with no light.** Coal is power now, and Tamara owns the coal. The young woman who wanted to be her father's heir has become something more dangerous.
- **A Harding child carried by a daughter of the White.** Both worlds will want it.
- **Jamie, alive in the White** with his mother and Morgana. The father of that child is on the wrong side of the world.
- **Everyone the chain ever took.** If Jamie is breathing in the White, so is the boy with the lantern: Edric, nine for four hundred years. And Otty, who walked down singing, and who, Morgana told Eldgard, is singing still. And the Hargraves of the Orchard, whose names are now on the Crown's record. Season 2's question writes itself: what do you owe the dead when they aren't dead?
- **Nixon and Tamara**: married to his cousin, holding his hand, nothing decided. Nathaniel and Alice: a kiss on Tyburn hill and no promise, because he's still Tamara's husband, and Tamara's hold on the city's coal rests on being Lady Harding. Three people in love, and the only marriage among them is the one keeping London warm.
