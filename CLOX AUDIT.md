- **Nixon, 9.14–15.** "Does neither" isn't a choice, it's a camera position. *Fix: log #5.*

### #1: Alice carries the child, and doesn't use it

- **Location in Script:** 9.5 → 9.13–15 → 10.9 → 10.15 → Rec.
- **Current Friction:** Wren tells Alice the child is held, then the scene cuts to a knife fight. Alice never thinks of it again. On the hill, "Go, if you want to. I'll be all right" costs her only her own loneliness.
- **The Emotional Upgrade:** Alice now holds the one argument that would keep her sister: *you're carrying Miriam's baby, Jamie's baby, stay.* She never uses it, not with the Lost One and not with Nathaniel. It shows in behaviour only:
  - 10.9: in the Gin Lane doorway, her hand goes flat over the coats at the belly before she turns back to her knife.
  - 10.15: as she says "Go," her hand starts toward the Lost One's coat, stops, and drops.
  - Nathaniel is never told.
- **The Mechanical Ripple Effect:**
  - 9.13: her choice to stay with Nathaniel now spends minutes of *his own niece or nephew*, and he doesn't know it.

## The Top Five

1. **Alice knows about Jamie's child from 9.5 and never uses it to keep her sister.** "Go" on the hill becomes the most expensive word she says all season.
