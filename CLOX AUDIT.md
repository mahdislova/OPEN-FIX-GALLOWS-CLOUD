## 1. Suppression and tension map

- **The faith was MISSING, not under-baked.** Before this pass the Orchard's religion only existed in the past tense: a wall of names and "walked down singing." Without a believer in the house, the theme of faith had no body in it. Ida gives it one, and her faith is the most frightening thing in the house because it is kind.
- **Nixon's loyalty was a debt to a powerful man.** Now it's love for the woman who raised him. The Ep 8 Orchard is no longer only a discovery about Eldgard. It's the moment the love curdles: "Of course, love. Isn't it beautiful?"
- **Where the dam breaks:** Ep 9, scene 8, on the servants' stair. Who has to be there: Nixon and Ida only, then Josephine at the top. Who must NOT be there: Nathaniel. If he saw it, the breakout couldn't happen. What can't be undone: the man who refused to fire on strangers kills the woman who raised him, with his hand, by accident.
- **Ida's own clox never opens for her,** and that's the point. Eldgard opens it in Ep 9, scene 16, an hour after her death, while Nixon is carrying him down the stair. The truth arrives with no possible audience except the man who can't tell anyone.

## 2. Character beats inside the set pieces

- **Newgate breakout (Ep 9, scene 9):** Nixon goes in with Ida's blood on his cuffs. He's still effective, but when Marsh asks "Is my sister well?" he lies, and Marsh notices the stains. The set piece now carries a second, silent scene underneath it.
- **The core (Ep 10, scene 4):** Nixon's offer, "They grew me for this," now has a second motive. Josephine sees the stains and knows it. The offer reads as penance as well as purpose.

## 3. Reveals that cost someone something

- **Otty's ninth step.** Who knew: Eldgard (eight, at the top of the stair) and Byron (who held the ribbon). Who kept it: Eldgard, from everyone, because he needed Ida believing. Who receives it: Nixon, on the landing, an hour after he has made Ida's belief permanent. What it does next: in the Record, Nixon writes IDA into Cole's diary at Marsh's hand.
- **Three layers.** London suspects nothing. Nixon knows what he did on the stair. Only Josephine knows *both* that he did it and why he's offering to walk. Nobody alive knows the whole of Ida.

## The Top Five

1. Kill Ida on the servants' stair by Nixon's panicked hand, and have Josephine say "She fell. Go."
2. Make the truth of Otty's walk the ninth step, Ida's lamp, and the light she followed, and let Eldgard tell it an hour too late.
3. Put "Isn't it beautiful?" at the top of the thirteen steps.
4. Make Marsh's first and last questions about his sister, and end with IDA in chalk on the wall.
5. Give Josephine the debt: she sees the stains at the core and knows why Nixon is offering.

## Answers chosen for the open questions

- **Who Ida reports to in Ep 9:** Josephine. Eldgard is in Newgate, and the breakout is for him.
- **Her name:** Ida is Orchard-born. "Marsh" is the name Newgate gave her brother. She gave Nixon her own surname.
- **Timeline:** Nixon lived below stairs with Ida from six to ten, then went to the charity school. The Ep 3 blood test at thirteen stays.
- **Ep 8 scene count:** "Of course, love" sits inside the Orchard scene, so no new scene was added.
