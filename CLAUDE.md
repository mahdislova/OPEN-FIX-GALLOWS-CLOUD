# The Green Gallows

`FULL NEW SEASON.md` is the season document. `green-gallows-season-tracker-new.html` is built from it.

Before reworking any scene, episode or character, read `WRITERS-BRAIN.md` and follow it. Its **Rulings** section outranks everything else in it.

When the showrunner reacts to a rework (likes it, hates it, changes it), add a dated ruling to `WRITERS-BRAIN.md` in their words, and adjust the dials if the ruling moves one.
