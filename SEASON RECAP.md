# The Green Gallows: The Season, Told as a Storybook

A newcomer's guide to the whole first season: what happened before it began, what happens in each of the ten episodes, where every character ends up, and how the season closes.

---

## Prologue: Four Hundred Years of Light

**The White and the frost child.** Beyond our world lies another one, called the White: endless snow, endless wind, white wolves, and no time at all. Whatever goes into the White stays exactly as it was when it went in, and nothing ever comes back out. In the hard winter of 1488 to 1489, the wall between London and the White wore thin at Tyburn, the gallows field where the Crown hanged its criminals. The Harding family were the Crown's hangmen there. Through that thin place wandered a little girl made of frost: the daughter of a being from the White named **Morgana**.

**The chaining.** **Alistair Harding** caught the frost child in a net and used her as bait. Her mother came for her: Morgana, nine feet of living blue ice with green fire running through her where a woman's veins would be. Alistair chained her in cold iron in a pit beneath the gallows, drove hooks through her body, and opened her wrist. Her blood ran green, burned with a clean white-green flame, and hardened into crystal. It was the first **gallowglass**, a light that never went out.

**The bargain.** Chained, Morgana offered Alistair a choice: *"Free me, and pay in light. Keep me, and pay in sons."* Either way, she said, her child went free that night. Alistair slid his silver **wolf-crest ring** onto his finger and chose **sons**. He let the frost child run into the White. He sealed the chain with his own blood, and the bargain became law. Every fifty years, on the night of the first snow, a member of the Harding family must walk down to the chain **of their own free will**, before midnight, and be taken by it. If nobody comes, the chain breaks. The first payment was Alistair's own son **Edric**, nine years old, the proud little boy who had held up the first lantern for his father. Edric carried that lantern down the stair alone.

**The Hargraves and the Orchard.** One of Alistair's cousins, a girl of nineteen named **Marian**, had watched the whole thing from the trees. That same winter she led the family's younger branch, the Hargraves, in a raid to free the prisoner, and swung a smith's hammer at the lock. Alistair cut her down at the chain. He hanged three thousand Hargraves on the gallows field by dawn. Their wives and children he walked down **thirteen steps** into a dungeon beneath the pit, and kept them there for four hundred years. The family called the place **the Orchard**, and the keepers' chalk tallies were headed **APPLES**. The Hargraves were cattle in the contract, bred, fed, and taught from the cradle that walking down to the chain was holy. Every fifty years, one of them did. Because the Hargraves were a branch of the Hardings, their blood was still **Harding blood**, which is all the chain cares about. The Orchard named its girls Marian, after the first one who tried.

**What gallowglass became.** Over the centuries the Hardings built the **Harding Works** over the old gallows field, and gallowglass came to power all of London: every lamp, tram, furnace and chandelier, carried through a grid of glass mains under the streets called **the Ring**. The Hardings told the world it grew from iron in the ground. Ground into dust, gallowglass becomes **glim**, the undercity's drug. People who take glim hear a woman crying in the light, which they call **the Weeping** and blame on a bad batch. It is Morgana singing a lullaby to her lost daughter. London has sung the same tune for four hundred years in church as the **Lantern Hymn**, with new words written over it.

**The Crown knows.** In **1689** the Crown discovered the truth. It chartered the **Royal Lantern Guard** to protect the Ring, signed a **sealed annex** to the Harding Charter admitting what the light really was, and locked it away. Every Lord Chancellor since has kept that annex in his desk. The Guard became London's elite. Its oath is *"Light the dark. Keep the flame,"* and its ranks run from **Wick** (recruit) to **Flame** (trooper) to **Lantern-Captain**. Its gear, all made at the Harding Works, includes the lantern gauntlet, whose **flash-burst** blinds a man and scatters wolves; **leap-boosters**, steam packs for rooftop jumps; the **quench-blade**, a glass sword that freezes what it cuts; and the **lantern rifle**, every shot of which is written into a **discharge log** bound into its case.

**Otty.** In **1839** the children of the Orchard stopped believing, and none of them would walk. So a true Harding walked instead: **Ottoline "Otty" Harding**, nineteen, went down the stair singing at first snow. Her page, a fourteen-year-old boy named **Byron**, held her blue ribbon. Her little brother **Eldgard**, eight, was told she had died of fever in Switzerland, and a brass plate in St Bride's church still reads **DIED ABROAD**.

**The Vykra, and a woman at the gate.** London's undercity has its own power: the **Vykra**, a guild of women who work as spies and knives for hire, run from a Shadwell laundry by **Mother Grise**. Their mark is a knot stitched inside the glove, and their poison, **winter's kiss**, kills with frost on the lips. Around 1859, the Vykra sent a nineteen-year-old assassin named **Josephine** to kill young Eldgard Harding. Byron, by then a Lantern Guard sergeant on night watch at the core gate, caught her because she had stopped to listen to the weeping. He let her go. A year later she married the man she had been sent to kill. Byron became the family's butler and never said a word, about her or about how he felt.

**The sons, and the spare.** Eldgard and Josephine had two sons. **Jamie**, born 1864, was raised from birth as the next payment due in 1889. **Nathaniel**, born 1868, was the second son and the spare. At five, Nathaniel followed Morgana's lullaby down to the core, and she sang his name back to him. Eldgard swung a lantern before the boy's eyes until he believed it had been a dream, but Nathaniel has heard her crying in the walls ever since. Meanwhile, in 1867, an Orchard man named **Tobias Marsh** escaped, was caught, and was locked in Newgate prison as a madman in **cell 1417**. In 1872 Eldgard took a six-year-old boy out of the Orchard, the son of the last girl named Marian, and told him his mother had died of fever. He put the boy in a charity school under the name **Nixon Hargrave**. When Nixon was thirteen, Eldgard tested his blood against a sliver of gallowglass, and it glowed green: *"Still good."* Eldgard was making a wager. The Orchard had failed in 1839, so he would raise a Hargrave free, with a name, a rank and a family to love, who would one day walk down the stair for that family **without being asked**. Nixon grew up to be Nathaniel's best friend, and never knew any of it.

**Jamie finds out.** Jamie learned what he was being raised for. For two years he visited old Marsh in Newgate every Sunday and learned the way down. He found the Orchard, chalked his initials **J.H.** over its door, and came up to ask his father how many had died. Then he fell in love with **Miriam Bounds**, a Gin Lane girl who ran glim for the Vykra. She became pregnant. Jamie planned to break the chain on the night of his brother's engagement ball, **2 November 1889**, and run away with her. He borrowed Byron's old Guard key and had it copied. He left the original with Byron and a note: *If I don't come up, give it to Nat.*

**The Bounds sisters.** Miriam had a little sister, **Alice**, four years younger. When they were girls, their stepfather drank and kept a belt. Miriam promised Alice in the dark under their bed that nothing would ever touch her while she was there. Then the belt came out, and Miriam never once moved to stop it. All she did was teach Alice to count the fence posts outside the window, one to eight, over and over: *"Eight posts and he's done."* The stepfather drowned drunk in the river one winter, and nobody looked for him. The sisters turned the counting game into a joke and never spoke of where it came from. Alice keeps that whole part of her life **"behind the fence."** She grew up believing that love means holding on to someone by any means, because nobody ever stood in front of her. Old **Wren**, the Gin Lane herb-woman, set Alice's broken arm when she was nine and never asked how it broke.

**Why 1889.** Otty paid in 1839. Fifty years later, the Tithe is due again. In four hundred years the first snow over Tyburn has always fallen on the longest night, **21 December**, so that's the night everyone counts toward. The season opens forty-nine days before it.

### The four rules of this world

1. **The Tithe.** Every fifty years, on the night of the first snow, a Harding must walk down to the chain of their own free will before midnight, or the chain breaks.
2. **Harding blood is the key.** Only Harding blood can hold, mend or open the chain, and Morgana's cold can't hurt it. Hargraves count as Hardings.
3. **Breaking it has a price.** If the chain breaks, Morgana goes free, London's light goes out for good, the White pours through until dawn, and one member of the family that chained her must go with her into the White. That person has to be given. She won't take them.
4. **The Lost One's cold costs her Miriam.** The creature living in Miriam's body (see Episode 1) freezes things when she feels too much or chooses to. Her touch can kill. Every time the cold comes out of her, one of Miriam's memories burns away. While the cold is in her, it keeps Miriam's body exactly as it was on the bridge.

## The Season

### Episode 1: "The Engagement"
*49 days to first snow. 2 November 1889.*

London glows green, and it is Alice Bounds's twentieth birthday, which her sister Miriam forgets while the two of them run glim through the tunnels under Gin Lane. Miriam wears a man's ring on a cord under her collar. When a buyer's belt buckle rings against an iron pipe, Alice flinches across the tunnel, and Miriam looks away. Mother Grise gives Alice her first Vykra contract from a client she won't name: get close to the second Harding son and report everything. That night is the ball where Nathaniel Harding's engagement to Tamara Lambrick is announced. The two of them, matched by their fathers, cheerfully agree to be very polite to each other for forty years. Alice gets in as a lamp-girl, and after tailing Miriam she has learned that her sister's secret lover is **Jamie Harding**. When Nathaniel's gauntlet shorts out, she fixes it with a hairpin in four seconds, counting fence posts under her breath. Josephine, watching from the gallery, notes that the girl "holds her wrist like a knife-girl." Eldgard humiliates Nathaniel in front of the room ("Toys after supper"), and Nathaniel bitterly tells Nixon, newly sworn in as captain, that he is "the son he actually wanted." Jamie slips away to the core with his copied key. He cuts at Morgana's lock with a crystal blade until it sticks in the iron, then presses his bleeding palm to it, and the city goes dark, because Jamie had paid Lambrick's man Ajax to cut the outer grid at that exact minute. In the black ballroom, only Nathaniel hears a woman crying inside the walls. Alice strikes a match ("Hear what, sir?"), refuses to give him her name, and slips the schematic of his gauntlet out of his coat. Below, Nixon's squad storms the core in flashes of strobe light, and Nixon shoots a masked saboteur at the lock. Under the mask is Jamie, wounded and alive. Eldgard sends Nixon away. Only young Cole, forgotten on the gantry above, sees Jamie still talking to his father. On Blackwood Bridge, Miriam waits for Jamie in her red mittens. He never comes. Frost climbs over her, a little girl made of frost appears at her side, and Miriam asks her something we can't hear. Alice finds her sister frozen solid inside a ring of frost. At dawn Eldgard tells the family Jamie fell in an accident, and orders Nixon to keep the secret, putting a leash on him for good. Nathaniel breaks down in the stables, and Nixon holds him with Jamie's blood still under his fingernails. In the city morgue, Alice touches her dead sister's hand, the frost melts, and the corpse's eyes move to her: *"Ali."*

### Episode 2: "The Wake"
*47 days to first snow.*

The thing in Miriam's body sits up on the morgue slab, freezes two attendants with a scream, and flees barefoot across the rooftops. It is Morgana's daughter, who crossed over on the bridge and put Miriam on like a coat. Alice finds her curled in a lamp tower, draining the crystal to get warm, and talks her down with the counting game, which the body remembers. Old Wren listens to a heart that barely beats, calls it shock, and promises the memories will come back. She knows better, and says nothing: a kind lie. While London buries Jamie in a closed coffin that feels far too heavy and too still in Nixon's hands, Alice takes "Mim" home. The creature meets bread, a kettle and a cat, and the cat freezes dead in her arms. So Alice makes rules: gloves always, no touching, and Miriam's red mittens on her hands. "You'll be yourself again soon, Mim." "Yes, Ali." It is the first lie, and the first kindness. At the funeral, Nathaniel hears the weeping under the Lantern Hymn and spots a small brass plate on the wall: Ottoline Harding, DIED ABROAD, the aunt nobody mentions. At the graveside he gives Alice his handkerchief, and she keeps it. We learn Alice's secret client: **Josephine**, a former Vykra herself, who now pays Grise double to keep her last son close and out of the Works. Nathaniel finds a spent **Lantern Guard rifle cartridge** at the core and asks his best friend to find out who fired it, so Nixon is now investigating himself. Cole writes everything he saw in his diary. Alice finds Jamie's ring on Miriam's cord ("He was supposed to come") and breaks into Jamie's bedroom, where she runs into Nathaniel doing the same thing. Hidden together in Jamie's wardrobe, they watch Eldgard sit on his dead son's bed and silently wind his watch. In a boot-tree they find Jamie's escape plan and a diagram of the chain, with a stair of thirteen steps marked *coal*. She gives Nathaniel one name: "Bounds." Inspector Felix Hawke, ordered by the Crown to drop the case, keeps the file anyway. He tracks the "Frost Killer" to Gin Lane, where the creature freezes his hand to a doorframe, and he tells Alice the surgeon said her sister had been **with child**. Wren tells her the cold took the baby. It is Wren's second, bigger lie. The creature walks to the Works gate and lays her palm on it, every lamp on the street leans toward her, and Nixon sees. Far below, for the first time in four hundred years, Morgana lifts her head: *"My daughter is here."*

### Episode 3: "Lanterns"
*42 days to first snow.*

Years ago, at a charity school, Eldgard pricked boys' fingers and touched the blood to gallowglass, and thirteen-year-old Nixon's glowed green: "Still good." Now Eldgard gives Nathaniel his first real job, re-arming the Lantern Guard for the Frost Killer hunt, and privately orders Nixon to bring the frost girl to the Works alive and tell no one, not even his son. So the son arms the hunt, his best friend leads it, and neither knows the target is the girl Alice is hiding. The creature, sensing a guilt that isn't hers, gives Alice a badly peeled orange for the birthday Miriam forgot. Tamara cracks her father's safe and copies six months of payments to someone called **A.F.**, ending on the night Jamie died. On the dark stairs, **Ajax Fink**, eating an apple, lets her pass. Grise gives Alice three days to bring something real on the Harding son, or the frost girl becomes the Vykra's business. So Alice visits Nathaniel, watches him leap across his foundry into a coal bin for an audience of one, and reads the night's sweep roster upside down: Gin Lane, tonight. Felix tells Nixon that Jamie's coat had a bullet hole in it, and hands him a Newgate slip: Jamie visited **prisoner 1417** every Sunday for two years. Tamara tells Nathaniel that A.F. is Ajax, which gives him the wrong suspect. At the Christmas crystal market the creature reaches for the glittering lanterns. Every crystal flares, the Guard swoops in, and in her terror she screams. Every crystal shatters, and four people die. In a bell tower, the kind young Wick **Cole** takes her hand when she begs, and starts to freeze. Nixon has the Frost Killer at his feet and a dying boy beside him. He carries Cole to safety and lets her go. Cole lives but loses two fingers, and Eldgard quietly lets Nixon know he knows about the shot in the core. Hidden in a tunnel under the Works, Alice tells her sister "You didn't mean it," the words Miriam used to whisper under the bed. The creature cries for the first time, in beads of ice. Miriam's guilt rises in her ("Ali. The belt. I should have—"), and Alice shuts it down: "That's behind the fence." Nathaniel tracks them down by listening for Alice's bootleg ghostlight, which is built from his own stolen schematic. The creature takes his bare hand, and nothing freezes, because he is Harding blood. He could hand the killer to his father and finally become the useful son. Instead he hides her, his first act against his father. Ajax leaves an apple core on Tamara's pillow as a warning. And the creature sniffs the air around Nathaniel: *"You smell like the man who keeps my mother."*

### Episode 4: "The Hearing"
*38 days to first snow.*

A flashback reveals the truth of the blackout. Lambrick paid Ajax to cut London's grid to shame the Hardings, but Jamie paid him **double** to do it at one exact minute, so that nobody would notice a Harding going down to the core in the dark. Nathaniel moves Alice and the creature into the Hardings' abandoned **glasshouse**, an old orangery where she makes snow for the first time and laughs like a child. Alice clings to her theory that Mim is "under" whatever came back, and when the creature tries to talk about the bed, Alice hands her an orange: "Eat that." The Crown's hearing on the Harding Charter is set, and Josephine sees that **Felix Hawke** will testify about the bullet hole, which could lead the Crown down to the core and to the Tithe waiting for her last son. Tamara turns to Nixon for protection. "I'll put a man on your door, miss." "I'd rather you were the man." Nathaniel builds Alice a real gauntlet and fits it to her arm strap by strap. Late one night the creature tells him, in Miriam's voice, that Jamie once called him "the only one of them worth saving." Grise orders Alice to hand over the frost girl. Alice refuses, and Grise snips the Vykra knot out of her glove: "Then you're no one's." Alice tucks Nathaniel's handkerchief where the knot used to be. On the coal docks the pair ambush Ajax. His **Black Gauntlet** turns out to be Nathaniel's own stolen prototype. They dangle him from a crane over the Thames, and he laughs: Jamie paid him, he never touched Jamie, and "your father went down after. Ask the Guard who fired." Alice begs Nathaniel to pull up their only witness. He lets Ajax drop into the river instead. In Newgate, Nixon finds old **Tobias Marsh**, who pinches his cheek ("Marian's chin!") and sings a rhyme: *seven in the Orchard… thirteen steps down… count the apples.* Felix finds the glasshouse, sees only a frightened girl who apologizes for his hand, and decides to tell the Crown that "someone made her." Josephine, hidden behind the garden wall, hears him. At the hearing, a veiled woman hands Felix a glass of water. He dies on the witness stand with frost spreading over his lips. The Frost Killer is blamed, the Crown orders her hunted "with every lamp in London," and sympathy swings to the Hardings. Tamara spots a Vykra knot inside the veiled woman's glove and pockets a dropped needle. Alice recognizes the poison as Vykra **winter's kiss**: her sister is being framed. Nixon finds his own rifle's discharge log recording one shot on 2 November and burns the page. Cole sees him do it and copies it from memory. Tamara walks into the laundry and joins the Vykra. In her dressing room, Josephine peels off a glove with a knot inside and washes frost from her fingers, and in the mirror, Byron is standing in the doorway.

### Episode 5: "Lantern Night"
*33 days to first snow. The midseason turn.*

The flashback shows the Hargrave raid of 1489: young Marian swinging a hammer at the lock, cut down as someone screams her name, three thousand hanged by dawn, and their families marched down thirteen steps. Eldgard finally brings Nathaniel "into the work," showing him a gleaming hall of vats where gallowglass supposedly grows "from Tyburn iron." The creature scoffs ("My mother isn't in a nice room"), and Nathaniel and Alice have their first fight, over family. Byron gives Nathaniel his old Guard key on a black ribbon, with Jamie's note: *If I don't come up, give it to Nat.* Eldgard privately orders Nixon to let his son into the core on Lantern Night, seal the door behind him, and keep the girl. On London's annual festival of light, while Lambrick and a newly scarred Ajax, fished out of the Thames, sabotage the grid with a coal mob, the heist goes in. The creature freezes the flooded drain into a glass road. Nixon whistles their childhood signal for *turn back*, and Nathaniel smiles and keeps going. Tamara, now Vykra, recodes the inner lock by ear while Alice holds her by the belt over a four-hundred-foot shaft. At the bottom, they see **Morgana** whole: nine feet of ice with hooks through her shoulders and hips, her green blood running in channels up into every lamp in London, singing the Lantern Hymn with no words. The creature grabs the chain, gets all of Miriam's memories back for one glorious second, and burns her mittens to smoke. Morgana tells Alice, "You have my daughter's hand. Give it back," and tells Nathaniel, "Your brother is not dead. He is cold." When Nathaniel touches the chain, his blood tightens it, and for one frame he sees a man standing still in endless snow. Then Eldgard steps out of the dark and tells him everything about the Tithe, including the price of breaking it: "I raised Jamie for it. Jamie ran. Now it's you." Nathaniel asks why his father never went himself, and gets only silence. The core door seals, and through the glass stands Nixon, following orders, mouthing his oath. In desperation the creature unleashes all her cold. Morgana screams, and across London every lamp blazes white and **bursts at once**. In the dark, a panicked crowd crushes people to death on Westminster Bridge while Lambrick bellows, "Remember who kept you warm!" Alice blasts the door open and reaches for Nathaniel, but he stays to "hear the rest," and she leaves without him. Eldgard tells his son that Morgana tells every Harding whatever will bring him to the lock ("She told me my sister was still singing") and begs him to help find another way. Nathaniel says yes. On a rooftop over the shattered city, the creature tells Alice: *"I'm going to free her. Even if all of them freeze."*

### Episode 6: "Coal"
*28 days to first snow.*

A flashback shows young Sergeant Byron catching the assassin Josephine at the core gate and letting her go, with a faded blue ribbon in his pocket. Now London is cold. Half its lamps are broken, Lambrick's coal carts fill the streets, prices triple, and a child freezes to death on Alice's doorstep. Father and son build the **Heartglass**, a crystal cage meant to hold the chain without a Harding death. It's everything Nathaniel ever wanted, until Eldgard mentions it will need "something of her blood to tune it." Nathaniel assumes he means Morgana's. Alice discovers that Wren was wrong: every use of the cold burns away one of Miriam's memories, and now the creature doesn't know Alice's birthday. Frightened, the creature begs to say something about "the belt" before it's gone, and Alice says "After." Then the creature offers a deal: let her sit by her mother for an hour, and she'll have Mim back. "You don't touch the lock." "Yes, Ali." It is the second lie, and Alice half knows it. Alice climbs to the Works window to tell Nathaniel, sees him happy at his father's side, and leaves without knocking. Tamara overhears her father planning to blow up the Tyburn conduit, where it meets his Pimlico gas main, on the night of the Charter vote, and learns two doctors are ready to sign her into a madhouse if she talks. She warns Alice, but Alice is going in anyway. Under a frozen canal bridge, Nathaniel finds his handkerchief kept in Alice's glove ("You kept it." "Bounds keeps things"), and they kiss for the first time. Byron confronts Josephine over Felix's murder. She has a poisoned needle in her sleeve and doesn't use it. But a Vykra tip that Grise bought from a tea-shop girl reaches her, and she passes it to Eldgard, who sends Nixon into the tunnels. She has spared Byron and sent him to his death without knowing it. Tamara's coded warning sends Nathaniel running, and Byron takes his old Lantern down from the wall and follows. In the conduit, Cole tells Nixon to confess to Nathaniel or he will. The creature grabs Nixon's bare wrist and he doesn't freeze, because he is Harding blood too. She tells him, "They told you your mother had a fever. She didn't." Alice and Nixon fight gauntlet to gauntlet. Then Ajax lights the charges early and the gasworks explodes. A mile away, the blast blows in the windows of Parliament mid-vote. In the rubble, Byron lifts a beam off Nathaniel and is crushed by it. Dying, he tells the boy that his mother killed the inspector, that everything came through the gate where he let her go, and finally, "You were always enough." The blue ribbon is in his fist. The creature uses the last of her cold to save four trapped children, then thanks Alice politely, like a stranger, because she has forgotten her name. The Crown suspends the Harding Charter and hands the Works to a board, with Lambrick on it. Nathaniel tells his mother Byron told him about Felix. She stops playing the piano: *"Then you know I love you."*

### Episode 7: "The Girl Who Isn't"
*21 days to first snow.*

We see Miriam's last morning. She was radiant, pregnant and terrified. She started a letter to Alice about their stepfather, scratched it out, wrote three short lines instead, and sewed them into her coat lining. Five weeks later, Alice finds them while mending the coat: *I'm leaving with him. I'm sorry, Ali. Don't follow me.* That night Alice confronts the creature, and fifteen years of silence break: "You left me under that bed. Every time." The creature, who still carries Miriam's guilt even after losing everything else, kneels and gives the apology Miriam never could: *I should have stood in front of you.* It is perfect, and that is how Alice knows. "Say my name." The creature can't. **"You're not her."** "No." Miriam was already gone when the creature opened her eyes. "I love you. I don't know whose that is anymore. I think it's mine." Alice throws her out. At breakfast Josephine announces the wedding in seven days. Touring the core under Crown control, Lambrick looks at Morgana and sees only profit ("She could be bled faster"), and Tamara reads a line in Eldgard's Heartglass notes: **Vessel: White-born**. On Blackwood Bridge, the outcast creature weeps, and her grief cracks open the thin place, letting wolves of the White through. They bow to her. On the Guard tower roof, Nixon finally confesses to Nathaniel that he fired the shot, and that Jamie was **still alive** when Eldgard sent him away. Nathaniel hits him once and walks away from fifteen years of friendship. Tamara sobers Alice up in a gin shop with one question: did Miriam ever stand in front of anyone the way this creature did? The wolves flood Whitechapel, freezing gas lamps and killing people in the street. Inside a music hall, a hundred people sing "Daisy Bell" to drown out the scratching at the doors, and Nathaniel and Nixon fight back to back an hour after their friendship ended. The creature walks into the street of wolves, stops them with one word in the language of wind, kills the biggest at the cost of another memory, and leads the rest back through the crack, which closes behind them. London will call her **the Frost Witch**. Alice reaches her and says, "I'm Alice. I'm your sister." Now everyone wants her: Lambrick tells the Crown to put her in the chain so it never needs a Harding again, and Josephine secretly buys her from Grise. Nathaniel breaks open Jamie's coffin and finds slag and a Bible: **no body**. At Jamie's portrait, the creature's hand goes to her belly as Miriam's love for him floods her body. Alice learns the Heartglass was built to cage her sister in her mother's place and hits Nathaniel for it, though he never knew. Then Tamara reveals that her father has a lunacy order signed and waiting, and the only way out of the asylum is to become someone's wife. "Marry her," says Alice, twice. On a rooftop, the creature relearns the counting game and keeps going past eight: *"Nine is the one where we get up."* Tamara gives Nixon a file with his name on it, kisses him a week before marrying his best friend, and leaves. He can't bring himself to open it.

### Episode 8: "The Wedding"
*14 days to first snow. 7 December.*

We see five-year-old Nathaniel at the core, Morgana singing his name, and Eldgard swinging a lantern until the boy believes it was a dream. On the night before the wedding, the creature makes Alice and Nathaniel rehearse the vows among the orange trees. They say every word and mean it, and stop before the kiss: "That part isn't ours." On the wedding morning, Josephine hands her son Grise's receipt: *Agent: Alice Bounds*. It is the first time he has ever seen her first name, and it's on a bill. Nathaniel storms off without hearing the rest. The creature sends Alice to the chapel anyway ("I'll be here"), her third lie. Nixon finally opens his file: *Hargrave, N. Cadet line of Harding. Reserve. Blood suitable.* He was never a charity boy, only a spare. In the vestry, Tamara offers to walk out of the church with him if he says one word. He can't, so she makes him hand her the ring "properly." At the chapel door, Josephine settles the bride's veil with a Vykra knot stitched inside her glove. Then Ajax appears with Bethlem attendants, a strait-coat, and a lunacy order for *Tamara Lambrick, spinster*, sealed by the Lord Chancellor sitting in the front pew. At the gallery rail, Alice gives Nathaniel one small nod. He says "I will" before the attendants can reach the altar, Nixon blocks the aisle with exaggerated politeness, hands over the ring ("Congratulations, miss"), and Vane has to concede: "This is addressed to a spinster. I see a wife." But while everyone watched the wedding, the Vykra took the creature from the glasshouse. She went willingly, because the only way into the Crown-held core was as a prisoner, and she left **DON'T FOLLOW ME** written in frost on the glass. Eldgard is arrested for his son's murder on his wife's word, with the wolf-crest ring still on his finger. At the gates, Vane orders the Lanterns to fire on the crowd. Nixon orders them to stand down, Cole walks between the rifles with his hands up, and Vane rips the lantern from Nixon's collar: "Keep the flame, Captain." "I am." Forty Lanterns throw theirs in the gutter and follow him. The newlyweds admit the truth to each other (she loves Nixon, and he knows), and Nathaniel goes to find Alice under the frosted pane: "What did you see up there?" "You, being brave." Alone, Nixon follows Marsh's rhyme down thirteen steps into **the Orchard**. He finds bunks, child-sized shackles, a wall of Hargrave names under the chalked word APPLES, the first name **MARIAN, 1489**, and the last name Marian too, his mother, dated four years after her supposed fever. Over the door is chalked **J.H.** In Newgate, Josephine admits she sold her husband to save their son, and says their thirty years were real. Eldgard warns her that Morgana will never let her child hang in her place: "Nobody refuses the door." "You would." In the core, the Heartglass closes around the creature as she tells her mother, "I've come to take your place." Morgana screams in grief, and it begins to **snow, two weeks early**. *Zero days.* Whatever is owed is owed by midnight.

### Episode 9: "The Snow Confession"
*First snow. Dusk to midnight.*

In a memory, Eldgard teaches little Jamie to skate and tells him that one day the city will need him to be very brave, while Josephine watches from the bank holding his scarf. In the snowy glasshouse, Nathaniel lashes out ("I learned your name on a bill"), and Alice finally tells him everything: she was paid at first, but after he flew into a coal bin for an audience of one she would have done it for nothing, and has been, for a month. "I'm married." "I know. I nodded." It's her plan now: midnight at the core. They need Eldgard's wolf-crest ring, and they need Nixon. Nathaniel finds Nixon's forty mutineers cooking sausages on gauntlets in an old bell foundry: "You shot my brother." "Yes." "You know we're cousins?" "Then you're family. God help you." Lambrick orders Tamara to deliver her husband to the chain, or, as a widow, go back into her father's custody. She steals the Works master key and his whole ledger instead, and stares Ajax down with a Vykra needle. Old Wren confesses she knew from the first night, and says the baby is **still there**, held by the cold just like Miriam's heart. Alice challenges Mother Grise by Vykra law, fights her among the steaming sheets, counts to eight, moves on **nine**, wins, and spares her, and the Vykra follow Alice to Tyburn. Josephine straps on the knives she hasn't touched in thirty years. The young alliance breaks Eldgard out of Newgate ("I came for your ring"), frees old Marsh into the snow, and Nixon carries the man who bred him across the rooftops. At the Works, wolves are forming out of the snowdrifts. Vane carries the Crown's secret 1689 annex in his pocket. The Heartglass is cracking because Morgana keeps pulling against her own hooks rather than accept her daughter's sacrifice. Lanterns, Vykra, Tamara and Nathaniel storm the courtyard against Crown troops, coal-men and wolves. Ajax beats Nathaniel down ("You should've pulled me up, Harding"), and **Eldgard steps under the killing blow**. Alice drives Ajax off but stays, giving up minutes her sister doesn't have. Dying in the snow, Eldgard confesses everything. Nixon's bullet hit Jamie in the shoulder, and Jamie would have lived. Eldgard **held his son's bleeding hand on the lock until the crack closed**, spending him to mend it, and filled the coffin with slag because the chain leaves no body. He took Nixon from the Orchard and raised him to walk down willingly for Nathaniel one day, and let him lock Nathaniel in so he'd see what the Tithe meant. Jamie found the Orchard and became his own man the night Eldgard told him the truth. Otty walked down singing. The Crown has known since 1689. "I could have gone down that stair myself. I raised a boy for it instead." He presses the wolf-crest ring into Nathaniel's palm: **"Don't be me.** The lock opens with what closed it: Harding blood, this ring, and a blade that's already in it." Nathaniel's free hand finds Alice's behind his back, and Eldgard dies with snow on his open eyes. Tamara and Nixon hold the stair under fire ("I want it on record I was married for twelve hours." "Noted"), and Nathaniel and Alice reach the core floor. Josephine stands at the Heartglass lever. "Nobody refuses the door." She pulls it.

### Episode 10: "The Tithe"
*The last night. Midnight to dawn.*

In 1489, Morgana offers Alistair the choice. He chooses "Sons," and little Edric carries the lantern down the stair alone. Now the lever drops and the door opens for Morgana, but she **refuses it**. She drags herself back onto her hooks until the glass around her daughter cracks: "Not her." Lambrick demands the boy instead, and the marshals start for Alice to force Nathaniel's hand. Alice smashes the Heartglass and pulls the creature out, half turned to crystal. Josephine fights her, Vykra against Vykra, until Nathaniel steps between them. Nixon offers himself as the sacrifice with open eyes: "They grew me for this the way they grew my mother." Nathaniel refuses: "No one goes in the chain again." Jamie's blade is frozen in the lock. Alice overloads the gauntlet Nathaniel built her against the iron and holds it there while her hand burns ("Us"), until the blade comes free. Nathaniel cuts his palm on it and presses his blood and his father's ring into the lock as midnight strikes. **The chain falls.** All of London goes dark, street by street, and this time the light never comes back. Under the Works, for the first time since he was five, nothing is crying in the walls. Morgana rises, says his name ("A Harding opened it"), and goes up through the roof to Tyburn hill as the White pours into the city. The floor splits, separating Alice and the freezing creature from Nathaniel and his mother. Nixon kills Ajax with a quench-blade (the apple rolls down the stairs) and clears the Guard stair. All night the Lanterns hold the bridges with lines of flash-bursts, the Vykra hold Gin Lane, and Tamara throws open every Lambrick coal depot to the city for free. **Cole** holds the barricade on Tyburn Road to his last cartridge, lets Alice pass with the creature in her arms, and dies there. His diary reads: *Held the road. Nobody past who shouldn't be.* Josephine tells her son to go to Alice, so unlike her that he stays to watch her instead, and follows her up the hill. In the coal vault, Tamara begs her father to come out, but Lambrick can't let go of his dying crystals, and the wolves come in. On Tyburn hill, the creature stands **in front of Alice**, between her mother and the city, the place Miriam never stood, and sings the hymn back in its original tongue. Morgana asks her to come home, and Alice, who has spent the whole season holding on, lets go: "Go, if you want to." Then the creature tells her the last thing she kept of Miriam. On the bridge, still alive, Miriam asked her: *"Go back to Ali. Stand in front of her. I never did."* The creature **chooses to stay**, and Morgana draws the White out of her like a splinter, leaving a mortal, shivering, warm girl. Then Morgana names her price: *"A Harding chained me. A Harding comes with me."* Nathaniel steps forward, and Alice steps in front of him: "Don't." **Josephine** steps past them both: "Not him. I'm a Harding. I chose the name." She asks that they not say she died of fever in Switzerland. "No," says her son. "They'll say your name." He lets her go. She blesses Alice ("Keep him out of the Works"), takes Morgana's hand, and walks into the White, and the wolves and the storm go with her. At dawn Alice tears the handkerchief in two to bind both their wounded hands and counts fence posts for him. He keeps going past eight to nine on his own, and for the first time says her name, **"Alice,"** with nobody paying for it. At Westminster, Tamara and Nixon lay out the evidence: the Heartglass folio, the ledger, the poisoned needle, and Cole's diary, which puts Nixon's own shot on the record. Nixon refuses a captain's lantern as a bribe ("The Guard will keep its own flame"). Nathaniel refuses a pardon that would bury the truth, and Tamara's coal forces the Crown's hand: the full record will be read into Parliament. Nathaniel dictates it name by name, from Edric to the Orchard to Otty, Jamie, Felix and his mother, while the city learns to live by candlelight. On Gin Lane, the creature, now warm, sneezes and is sick in a basin, and under Alice's hand a heartbeat stirs: **Jamie's child**, alive again now the cold is gone. "Is that me?" "No. That's someone else." In the White, Josephine finds Jamie standing frozen in the snow, kept but not living, with little Edric still holding his lantern far behind him. She sits down beside her son. *Day one.*

## Where Everyone Ends Up

- **Nathaniel Harding**: Opens the lock, ends the four-hundred-year bargain, and puts out London's light for good. Branded "the man who put out London," he refuses the Crown's silence and has the whole truth read into the official record. Married on paper to Tamara, he finally says Alice's name and kisses her on Tyburn hill. He drops his father's ring into a dead grate.
- **Alice Bounds**: Learns the difference between letting go of someone who chooses to live and standing in front of someone who chooses to die. Her hand is burned from the lock. She leads the Vykra on her own terms, stitching a knot nobody taught her, and Grise pours her tea. She keeps her sister, and she has Nathaniel's name on her lips with nothing promised.
- **The Lost One**: Gives up her mother, her cold and her immortality to stay with Alice. A mortal, warm girl now, she will never make snow or hear her mother sing again. She carries **Jamie and Miriam's baby**, whose heart has started beating again.
- **Nixon Hargrave**: Learns he was bred in the Orchard as the family's spare, and that his shot didn't kill Jamie. He offers his life on purpose and is refused. He leads the Lantern Guard as captain on his own terms, chosen by his own men, not the Crown. He stands beside Tamara, with nothing decided.
- **Tamara Lambrick, Lady Harding**: Heir to her father's coal empire and wife of Nathaniel. She exposes her father under her own name, forces the Crown to publish the truth, and in a London with no gallowglass, holds the city's only heat. She keeps her marriage on paper with Alice's blessing, "until the coal runs out," and loves Nixon.
- **Cole Pruett**: Dies holding the Tyburn Road barricade at seventeen, a real Lantern at last. His diary, which he kept from the first night, puts the truth on the record. His Wick lamp, lit with a plain match, now hangs on the Guard tower roof.
- **Eldgard Harding**: Dies in the snow taking a blow meant for his son, after confessing everything. His last words: "Don't be me."
- **Josephine Harding**: Gives herself to the White in her son's place, paying the family's last debt. There she finds Jamie, frozen and kept, and sits down beside him. She is not dead, but not living either, and will not come back.
- **Byron**: Died in the Pimlico explosion saving Nathaniel. His blue ribbon now hangs on Otty's corrected brass plate.
- **Jamie Harding**: Never died in the usual sense. The chain took him into the White, where he stands frozen exactly as he was on the lock. His child lives.
- **Miriam Bounds**: Died on Blackwood Bridge. Her last act was asking the frost girl to keep the promise she had broken, and the frost girl kept it.
- **Morgana**: Free after four hundred years. She refused her daughter's sacrifice, let her stay, took Josephine as the price, and returned to the White.
- **Lord Lambrick**: Couldn't let go of his crystals and died in the vault when the wolves came in. His ledger convicted him after death.
- **Ajax Fink**: Killed on the core stair by Nixon's quench-blade, mid-laugh, his apple rolling down the steps.
- **Mother Grise**: Survives everything, as she always does, beaten by Alice and pouring her tea.
- **Inspector Felix Hawke**: Poisoned on the witness stand by Josephine in Episode 4. The Record names him and his killer, and the Frost Killer posters come down.
- **Lord Chancellor Vane**: Forced to read the truth into Hansard. He locks the 1689 annex back in his drawer anyway. The Crown survives.
- **Tobias Marsh**: Freed from Newgate, lucid at last, he reads the names of the Orchard's dead off the wall.
- **Old Wren**: Her kind lies gave Alice five weeks of a sister. Her second lie said the baby was gone when the cold was only holding it.
- **Edric, Otty, and the Hargraves of the Orchard**: Everyone the chain ever took is standing in the White, exactly as they went in. Their names are now on the record.

## The End of the Season

The bargain is over. London, which ran for four hundred years on a woman's blood, has gone dark for good. It warms itself by coal and tallow now, candle passed to candle through broken windows, a surgeon operating by six candles with shaking hands. Power has shifted to whoever controls the coal, and that is Tamara. The truth (the chained mother, the Orchard, the Hardings' dead sons, the Crown's signed annex) has been read into Parliament and sold on every newsstand. The city hates the man who switched off its light, and knows exactly what that light cost. Children on Gin Lane still skip to the old rhyme, but with a new ending: *nobody's in the cellar tonight.*

Three people love one another without a promise among them: Nathaniel and Alice, kissed on Tyburn hill, and Nixon and Tamara, side by side on the Guard roof. The only marriage among them is the paper one keeping London warm. A daughter of the White, now human, carries a Harding child that both worlds will want. And on the far side of the snow, everyone the chain ever took is still standing exactly as they went: Edric with his lantern, Otty, the Orchard's "apples," Jamie, and now Josephine beside him. Morgana said Jamie was "cold, not dead," and she meant it to the letter. Nobody comes back from the White. The question waiting for Season 2 is what you owe the dead when you know exactly where they are.

The card that counted down every episode comes up one last time, and it doesn't count down anymore: **DAY ONE.**
