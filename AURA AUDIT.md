### What was implemented

- **#1, the reputation line, moved from 5.17 to 5.6.** Cole is holding the Westminster Bridge barricade at 5.19 and can't also be at the core door two scenes earlier. In 5.6 he walks in with the duty roster just after Eldgard has given the order to seal the door on Nathaniel: "Lantern Night at the Works, sir. You've never once disobeyed an order, have you?" "No." It lands harder there, because we've just watched the order he's about to obey.

**8.14, "I am." (10 → 14).** It's missing reputation and a rule.

Reputation is there (Grise: "Four minutes"), and so are the witnesses. She ends the season with her moment.

As Alice passes with her sister in her arms, Cole, out of charge, lifts his two-fingered hand in the Wick's salute he smacked himself in the eye with in the pilot (1.5), and gets it right this time. ### #1: "I am." gets its price

- **Location in Script:** 5.17 and 8.14.
- **Current Friction:** It's the season's mutiny, and it scores 10: we never heard Nixon was a man who obeys, and we don't know what refusing costs.
- **The Fix:**
  - At 5.17, Cole beside him: "You've never once disobeyed an order, sir." "No."
  - At 8.14, Vane, before he rips the lantern off: "A Lantern who refuses a lawful order before the Crown forfeits his lantern and his name."
  - Cost: +13 sec in total. Ep 5 has headroom. **Ep 8 is one-for-one:** the marshals' inventory at 8.13 shrinks from "his watch (Jamie's…), his cane and his collar studs" to "Jamie's watch, wound every night since the funeral" (−5 sec), and "Nixon walks out of the chapel still holding the empty ring box" folds into the walk-out (−8 sec).

  - 5.17 rises to 12.

  - 9.8's "Marian" lands on a man we've seen break once already.
- **Effort / Impact:** Low / **High**

### #2: Episode 3 ends warm, with dread

- **Location in Script:** 3.16 and 3.18.
- **Current Friction:** It's the third reveal ending in a row, and it spends "You smell like the man who keeps my mother" as a button when it would cut deeper mid-scene.
