# The Green Gallows — Character-Track Audit: The Sisters

Audit of `FULL NEW SEASON.md` (Oct 3, revised Oct 10). 13 November 2026.

## How to read this

Each sister is followed through all ten episodes in her own voice. Each narration reports only what she saw, heard or was told on the page. Where the outline doesn't say how she got from one scene to the next, or why she did something, the narration doesn't fill the gap. It stops and marks it like this: **[→ A-05]**. That ID is in the issue register at the end.

- References use the outline's own numbering: **Ep 7 sc 3**.
- IDs: **A-** Alice, **Mi-** Miriam, **L-** the Lost One, **E-01** your worked example.
- **SA** means the existing `SHOWRUNNER_AUDIT.md`. Where a finding overlaps one of its issues, the SA ID is given so nothing gets counted twice. Most findings here are new, because staying with one person turns up different holes.
- I checked every suspected problem against the later episodes. Anything a later scene fixes has been dropped. A short list of suspicions that turned out fine is at the end of Part 3.

---

## The short version

The sisters are the best-built thing in the season. Alice's denial is tracked honestly from start to finish. The Lost One has three "Yes, Ali" lies that work as a clear spine. The reveal is the best scene in the document. The two DON'T FOLLOW MEs are a superb piece of structure: Alice could never follow Miriam's, and she breaks the Lost One's. Keep all of it.

Following the two of them scene by scene exposes ten things the episode view hides:

1. **The name system breaks in the peak scene.** The Lost One can't say Alice's name, then says "Ali" four lines later (Ep 7 sc 3). She says it again in Ep 8 sc 4. On Tyburn hill she says she has "spent" Miriam's name for Alice and then speaks it in the next breath (Ep 10 sc 16). **L-09, L-10.** The fix makes the hill scene stronger, not just tidier.
2. **Alice's biggest change happens in someone else's scene.** The moment she goes from holding on to letting go, when she carries her sister to her mother, is reported from inside Cole's barricade scene (Ep 10 sc 10). **A-23.**
3. **Alice plans to put out London's light without ever deciding to.** In Ep 6 sc 4 she says the dark kills people. By Ep 9 sc 2 the plan to open the lock is hers, and by Ep 10 sc 5 she says "Us." No scene in between changes her mind. **A-20.**
4. **Alice's reconciliation is set off by Tamara,** who tells her about something Alice saw herself and Tamara didn't see (Ep 7 sc 8). **A-14.**
5. **Alice already knew Miriam was leaving.** She read "an escape plan for two" in Ep 2 sc 13. That takes the edge off the Ep 7 letter unless the letter's news is re-aimed at "Don't follow me." **A-07.**
6. **Alice never learns what Josephine did to her sister.** That covers the frame-up (Ep 4 sc 15), the sale (Ep 8 sc 8), and the poisoning of Felix. Then she blesses Josephine on the hill (Ep 10 sc 17). **A-10.** Grise can fix this with one speech.
7. **The child is the sharpest knife in the drawer, and nobody picks it up.** Wren tells Alice the baby is "still there" (Ep 9 sc 5). Alice then says "Go, if you want to" (Ep 10 sc 15) without the outline ever weighing that she is also letting Miriam's child go into the White. **A-21.**
8. **The Lost One never says goodbye to her mother,** and isn't placed in the dawn scene at all (Ep 10 sc 16 to 18). **L-16, L-17.**
9. **The Lost One goes missing for two key stretches:** while her wolves kill people in Whitechapel (Ep 7 sc 6 to 12), and through Eps 3 and 4, when she lives directly under and beside her mother and never once tries to reach her. **L-12, L-03.**
10. **Miriam's death has an answer on the page that nobody uses.** The frost reaches her before the child does, and the outline's own cause and effect points to Jamie's crack. Making her request a bargain (her body for a promise) gives Miriam the one moment in her life when she didn't flinch. **Mi-01** (SA M-11).

**Handover.** She ends in the city morgue on the night of 2 November, with her sister's corpse saying her name (sc 20). **Issues exposed**
- **A-01 (minor).** Her second want is answered at dusk (sc 7). She then spends the ball in the same room as the man her sister is leaving her for, and never sees him (sc 10: "only Nathaniel sees him go"). Nothing gives her a reason to go to the bridge at midnight (sc 13 to 17). One fix covers both: let *her* see Jamie slip out too. She follows the only lead she has.
- **A-03 (minor).** Grise's order was "Report everything" (sc 6). She steals the schematic (sc 13), never hands it in, and builds her own ghostlight from it (Ep 3 sc 16). The withholding is a good character choice, but the page never makes it one. See Ep 3.
- **Holds:** the flinch at the buckle and Miriam looking away (sc 2), on the very morning, as Ep 7's cold open later shows, that Miriam tried to write the apology. The match burning to her fingers (sc 13).

## Ripe for turmoil: the hard choices the outline has set up and not yet used

Ranked by return on the smallest change.

1. **"Go" costs Miriam's child (A-21).** Ep 9 sc 5 loads it. In Ep 10 sc 15 Alice has to choose knowing her niece or nephew goes into the White with her sister. That turns the season's quietest letting-go into its heaviest, using two gestures and no new lines.
2. **Alice chooses the dark, with a dead child in her doorway (A-20).** She's the one character who has watched a Gin Lane child freeze (Ep 6 sc 2), and she argued for the light because of it. 3. **Miriam gives her body (Mi-01).** Change the bridge from a request to a bargain and Miriam, who "flinched every single time it mattered," gets the one moment in her life when she didn't. Then, in Ep 9 sc 14, Alice understands that Jamie's crack, his escape plan for two, is what froze her sister, while she is kneeling at his brother's back. The person she's holding is related to the reason she lost her sister. She doesn't let go.
4. **The Lost One spends Alice to keep Miriam's promise (L-08).** To stand in front of strangers (four children, a hundred in the music hall) she pays with Alice's name and face. She keeps the promise in general by spending the person it was made about. Make it conscious, with one look at the river stair, and her season has an engine of its own. The hill then becomes the first time she stands in front of *Alice* herself.
5. **The daughter who ran (L-18).** She was the bait, and she fled while her mother took the hooks. "Because I ran" gives the Lost One her own flinch, and makes Ep 10's two refusals ("Not her," "Not him") about mothers who won't let a child pay twice.
6. **Alice's own dead.** The market route (Ep 3) and "Get out" (Ep 7) are her choices, with body counts, and she never reckons with either. The A-14 repair sends her into Whitechapel to face what her words let through. That's a far stronger reason to choose her sister than Tamara's argument.
7. **What she calls her (L-19).** In a season that ends with a name said "when nobody has paid for it," the sisters' story ends without one. Giving it costs one exchange.
8. **The goodbye on the hill (L-16)** and **Morgana's look in the core (L-15).** Two short beats, and between them the mother's letting go comes first and teaches the sister's.
9. **Her dead, in the Record (L-13).** Nathaniel's dictation names everyone the house killed. The Lost One killed a chestnut-seller in the market (Ep 3 sc 11), and in Ep 7 sc 2 she comes home happily chattering about a man who sells chestnuts out of a drum. Make that echo deliberate, and give one line of the Record to a name she asks him to add. It's optional, but it would be the Record's most surprising entry.
